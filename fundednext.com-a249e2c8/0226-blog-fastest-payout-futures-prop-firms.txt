2. My Funded Futures
My Funded Futures is known for rapid payout approvals once a trader submits a withdrawal request. In many cases, payout requests are approved instantly. When a manual review is required, approval typically takes 6–12 business hours on weekdays.
Additional payout timing details:

Payout requests are generally processed within 1–3 business days (weekends excluded).
Requests submitted on weekends are usually processed between Monday and Wednesday.
For live accounts, requests submitted before 11:00 AM EST may be processed the same day; requests submitted later are typically handled the next business day.

Focus

3. Topstep
Topstep follows a structured, multi-step payout process. After a trader submits a payout request, the request typically takes 1–3 business days for approval. Once approved, payouts are issued based on the selected payment method.
Estimated payout delivery times after approval:

Wise: 1–3 business days
ACH: 1–3 business days
Wire / SWIFT: 3–5 business days
International Wire / SWIFT: up to 5–10 business days

These timelines are estimates and may be extended due to additional reviews, payment provider processing times, or bank holidays. Most banks do not process payments on weekends or federal holidays.
What Traders Get

Clearly structured and well-documented payout rules
Predictable delivery timelines with advance estimates per payment method
Multiple payment methods with transparent timelines

How It Compares
Topstep delivers reliable and predictable payouts, but its approval and payment process is longer compared to firms that prioritize rapid payout speed. It does not offer a guaranteed 24-hour payout window or compensation model, making it better suited for traders who value structure and consistency over immediate access to payouts.
4. Alpha Futures
Alpha Futures offers structured payout processing with clearly defined timelines across multiple payment methods. Once a trader submits a withdrawal request, the firm processes payouts within 24–48 business hours.
Key Payout Details:

Payouts processed within 24–48 business hours
Funds may take up to 10 business days to reflect in a bank account, depending on the payment method selected
Processing occurs on business days
Confirmation provided after payout approval

Payment Method Timelines (After Approval)

International Wire: 2–10 business days
SWIFT: 1–2 business days (in some cases up to 5)
ACH: 1–3 business days
Wise / Rise: Within 24 business hours after approval

How It Compares
Alpha Futures delivers relatively fast internal payout processing, with most requests handled in just a few hours. However, total fund receipt time depends on the selected payment rail and may extend to several business days. Unlike FundedNext Futures, Alpha Futures does not publish a compensation-backed payout guarantee if delays occur, placing it slightly behind firms that formalize payout accountability with financial guarantees.
5. Tradeify
Tradeify offers futures prop trading accounts with a clearly structured withdrawal system. Once a payout request is approved, funds are immediately deducted from the trading account. Payment processing then begins.

Payment issuance: Typically within 24–48 hours after approval.
Requests submitted outside normal business hours (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM EST or federal holidays) may take up to 72 hours to process.
Lightning accounts include a faster 24-hour payout guarantee.

Receiving Funds

Rise (primary method):

Bank transfers usually take 1–3 business days.
Crypto transfers are often completed within hours.

Plane (alternative method):

Bank-to-bank transfers may take up to 7 business days.

Overall, the total time from payout request to funds reaching a trader’s bank account is typically 2–5 business days using Rise, or up to 10 business days using Plane, depending on the payment method selected.
How It Compares
Tradeify emphasizes account structure, risk controls, and scaling options rather than ultra-fast access to payouts. While reliable and transparent, its multi-step payout flow makes it less suitable for traders whose primary priority is immediate or same-day payout speed.
