MCP Connector PolicyThis policy explains what data the FundedNext MCP connector (the “Connector”) accesses, how it is used and shared, and how long it is kept. It is provided by FundedNext (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions, contact [email protected].This Addendum complements the FundedNext Privacy Policy and applies specifically to your use of the Connector. In the event of any conflict, the more specific terms set out in this Addendum shall prevail for matters relating to the Connector; for all other matters, the FundedNext Privacy Policy continues to apply.Read-only, and only your own data.
The Connector retrieves information; it never trades, moves money, or changes your account. It can only ever access the data belonging to the account you sign in with.19.1 What the Connector isThe Connector lets you ask an AI assistant about your own FundedNext account data. You authorize it through a standard OAuth 2.0 sign-in; your password is never shared with the assistant, and you can revoke access at any time from your FundedNext account or the AI assistant.19.2 Data we access (only your own)When you ask a question, the Connector reads only the data belonging to your authenticated account, which may include:Profile basics (name, email, country) and account/login identifiersTrading accounts, their plans, rules, status and balancesYour trade history and trading performance (CFDs and Futures)Payout, withdrawal and wallet historyKYC/verification status and decision reasons (status only — not the contents of your uploaded documents)Certificates, competition participation/results, coupons and notificationsThe Connector enforces strict per-customer scoping: it is not possible to retrieve another customer's data through it.19.3 What we do not accessWe do not read your AI conversation history, chat memory, or files you have shared with the assistant. We do not collect data beyond what is needed to answer your request. We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data to train AI models.19.4 Legal basis for processingData accessed and processed through the Connector relies on the same legal basis described in Section 6 of the FundedNext Privacy Policy, applied according to the purpose of the specific request, principally performance of a contract (to provide the Services you have requested) and legitimate interests, and legal obligation where applicable. No separate or additional legal  basis applies solely because access occurs through the Connector.19.5 How your data is used and sharedTo answer your question: The data the Connector retrieves is returned to the AI assistant (for example Anthropic's Claude) so it can compose a response to you. By using the Connector you understand that the requested account data is shared with that assistant for this purpose, governed by the assistant provider's own privacy terms.Knowledge base: Rule and policy answers use the FundedNext knowledge base. Building its search index uses Amazon Web Services (AWS Bedrock) as a sub-processor; only non-personal FAQ/policy text is processed for this — not your account data.Sub-processors: We rely on Amazon Web Services (hosting and embeddings) and the AI assistant provider you connect (e.g. Anthropic).
