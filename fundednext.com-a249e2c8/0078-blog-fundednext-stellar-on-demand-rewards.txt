What Is On-Demand Rewards?
On-Demand Rewards is an optional checkout add-on available for FundedNext Stellar CFDs challenges. Traders can add it to a Stellar Lite, Stellar 2-Step, or Stellar 1-Step challenge at checkout for base price + 5%.
It solves one specific problem: reward timing. Most reward structures run on a fixed cycle; you wait for the cycle to close before accessing what you have earned. With On-Demand Rewards, that changes. Once your account is up 2% and maintaining the 40% consistency rule, you can request a payout on any qualifying day and receive it within 24 hours.
How On-Demand Rewards Works
The process has four steps:

Add it at checkout. Select On-Demand Rewards when purchasing a Stellar Lite, Stellar 2-Step, or Stellar 1-Step challenge. Cost: base price + 5%.
Get your account to 2%. Requests open once your account is up 2%.
Maintain the 40% consistency rule. Your best single trading day must stay within 40% of your total performance on FundedNext Accounts only.
Request on any qualifying day. No fixed schedule. No waiting for a cycle date.
Receive it same-day, end of day. The payout is disbursed within 24 hours after requesting.

There is no daily cap on performance and no buffer requirement. The system is designed to be easy to understand and easy to self-check.
Why the 95% Reward Share Matters
The 95% reward share applies from your very first performance reward, not after hitting a milestone or clearing a scaling threshold. If your eligible performance is $1,000, you keep $950.
This is not a secondary benefit attached to faster access. It is a core part of what the add-on delivers. For traders who consistently earn and request rewards, the difference between a 95% share and a lower one compounds over time. That extra percentage stays with the trader.
The One Condition: 40% Consistency Rule
On-Demand Rewards has a single condition that applies to FundedNext Accounts only: your best single trading day must remain within 40% of your total performance.
If your total performance stands at $1,000, your highest single day should not exceed $400. That is the consistency check. Before submitting a request, confirm your best day is within 40% of your total. There is no daily cap on performance and no buffer to maintain. One rule, clearly stated.
Which Challenges Include On-Demand Rewards?
On-Demand Rewards is available as an optional checkout add-on on the following FundedNext CFDs challenges:

Stellar Lite
Stellar 2-Step
Stellar 1-Step

It is available globally, including the USA, and applies to Stellar challenges only.
Who Is This Add-On For?
On-Demand Rewards is built for traders who trade consistently, with steady performance spread across multiple trading sessions, not single concentrated gains. If your account regularly reaches the 2% threshold, the add-on gives you access to what you have earned when you have earned it, rather than on a schedule set by a fixed cycle.
If your trading tends toward high single-day performance, track the 40% consistency rule carefully before submitting a request.
Trade consistently. Take payouts on demand. Keep 95%.
To add On-Demand Rewards to your next Stellar challenge, select it at checkout.
