CFDsFuturesLabsRewardsMoreENLoginCFDsFuturesLabsRewardsSearch forex termsCFDs GlossaryAsset ClassAsset ClassWhat is an Asset Class in Trading?An asset class is a group of similar financial instruments you can trade, such as forex, commodities, indices, cryptocurrencies, stocks, or options. For CFD (Contract for Difference) traders, understanding different asset classes is essential for making informed decisions, managing risk effectively, and building a diversified strategy.Each asset class responds differently to economic events, interest rate shifts, or geopolitical changes. For instance, Forex traders might look at the EUR/USD pair for short-term volatility, while others may turn to XAUUSD during uncertain economic periods. With CFDs trading, traders do not own the underlying asset, they speculate on its price movement, gaining access to multiple asset classes from a single platform.What Are the Different Types of Asset Classes?Here are the main types of asset classes available in CFDs trading:Forex (Currencies): Trade currency pairs such as EURUSD, GBPJPY, or USDJPY. The Forex market is the most liquid and active market globally, offering opportunities around the clock based on macroeconomic news, interest rates, and market sentiment.Indices: Speculate on the overall performance of a market sector or country through index CFDs like the S&P 500, NASDAQ 100, or US30. These reflect the collective movement of top-performing stocks.Commodities: Access global markets for physical assets like gold (XAUUSD), crude oil (WTI), or natural gas (NATGAS). Commodities often respond to inflation, geopolitical tension, and supply-demand shifts.Equities (Stocks): Trade CFDs on individual company shares such as Apple (AAPL) or Tesla (TSLA), without owning the actual stock. Perfect for traders who follow earnings reports, company news, or sector trends.Crypto Asset Class: Speculate on the price movements of digital currencies like Bitcoin (BTCUSD) or Ethereum (ETHUSD). These markets are known for high volatility and 24/7 availability.Asset Class ExamplesAt FundedNext, CFD traders can explore a wide range of asset classes—whether it's major Forex pairs, popular indices, safe-haven commodities, trending equities, or fast-paced crypto markets. For example, a trader might scalp the GBPUSD pair during the London session or swing trade XAUUSD based on global inflation data.Understanding how each asset class behaves helps traders refine their strategies based on market conditions, risk tolerance, and time horizon—making them better equipped to navigate today’s dynamic market.Other Glossary TermsAAccount BalanceAccount balance refers to the amount of funds currently available in a trader’s account.Ask PriceIn CFDs (Contract for Difference) trading, the ask price is the price at which traders can buy an asset.ArbitrageArbitrage trading involves buying an asset at a lower price on one platform and selling it almost simultaneously at a higher price on another.Automated TradingAutomated trading, also called auto trading or algorithmic trading is the process of executing trades based on pre-programmed rules and conditions.Asian SessionThe Asian trading session runs approximately from 2:00 AM to 11:00 AM (GMT+3).Start yourFundedNext challengeThousands of traders are already getting rewarded by FundedNext.
