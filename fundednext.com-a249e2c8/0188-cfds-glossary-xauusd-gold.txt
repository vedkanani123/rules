CFDsFuturesLabsRewardsMoreENLoginCFDsFuturesLabsRewardsSearch forex termsCFDs GlossaryXAU/USD (Gold)XAU/USD (Gold)What is XAUUSD (GOLD) in Trading?XAUUSD is the price of GOLD quoted in US Dollar . XAU is the code for one troy ounce of GOLD, and USD is the Dollar. The XAUUSD Price shows how many US Dollars needed for one ounce of GOLD. People also search for XAU TO USD, GOLD USD, or FX GOLD when checking this quote.Why It Matters for CFDsIt is one of the most traded metal CFDs, often called GOLD Forex on platforms.Moves can be sharp around news about inflation, interest rates, or the US Dollar. It also often considered a safe-haven assetTraders watch the US Dollar INDEX (DXY) and global risk sentiment before entering XAUUSD trades. Because When the US dollar rises, gold prices tend to drop – and vice versa.How the Price WorksIf the USD strengthens, GOLD often falls because fewer Dollars are needed to buy one ounce. If the USD weakens, GOLD often rises. For example, you buy a GOLD CFD at a XAUUSD price of 3900.00 and later close at 3912.50.The move is +12.50 US Dollars per ounce, multiplied by your contract size. Similarly, if the US Dollar strengthens, currency pairs may also react. For instance, EURUSD could drop from 1.10201 to 1.09850 as the USD gains strength.Where to Check ItMost CFD platforms display the live XAUUSD price, often alongside the USD Index Chart or Dollar Index Today for context.Other Glossary TermsXXAG/USD (Silver)XAGUSD represents Silver priced in US Dollars, showing how many USD are needed to buy one ounce of Silver.
