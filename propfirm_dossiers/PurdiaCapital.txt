SECTION 5 — MODEL-BY-MODEL RULES
================================================================================
--- 5A. PRO EVALUATION (Intraday-trailing) ---
- TMD trails open equity incl. unrealized highs in real time (strictest).
  Example 100K: $3K trail from every intraday peak; open dip below floor = fail.
- 5 min trading days; hit target within TMD.
--- 5B. BEGINNER/EOD EVALUATION ---
- EOD trailing: steps at EOD close on new highs only; intraday gains ignored.
  Same 5-day gate; kinder wick handling.
--- 5C. SIM FUNDED -> TEMP SIM -> LIVE (LFA) ---
- Sim Funded: same trailing engine (never stops trailing in sim, even past
  breakeven). 10 min days + 5 profitable days to qualify.
- LFA: static breakeven+$100 (never trails; profits add, losses/payouts subtract).
- IFA: starts directly in Sim Funded; same funded gates from day one; no resets.
- Conduct/identity limits + enforcement per Trading Policy.
