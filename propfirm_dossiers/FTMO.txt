SECTION 5 — MODEL RULES (OFFICIAL, VERBATIM SUBSTANCE)

----- A. CFD 2-STEP CHALLENGE (flagship) -----
Path: Challenge -> Verification -> KYC/KYB + FTMO Account Agreement ->
FTMO Account (2-Step).
PHASE 1 — FTMO Challenge
- Profit Target: 10% of Initial Simulated Capital, all positions closed.
  $100,000 example: pass at $110,000 balance.
- Max Daily Loss: 5% of Initial Simulated Capital. Recalculated daily at
  00:00 CE(S)T as: midnight balance minus 5% of initial. Day-1 $100k:
  floor $95,000. Day-2 if midnight bal $102k: floor $97,000.
  Breach = equity below the day's floor.
- Max Loss: STATIC 10% of Initial Simulated Capital. $100k floor = $90,000
  for the whole phase (does not trail). Applies Challenge, Verification,
  AND subsequent FTMO Account (2-Step).
- Min Trading Days: 4 per phase. A Trading Day = any day 00:00:00-23:59:59
  CE(S)T on which at least one position is OPENED (not merely held).
  Holding overnight does NOT count extra days unless a new open occurs.
- Time limit: UNLIMITED.
PHASE 2 — Verification
- Same daily 5% / static 10% / 4 min days / unlimited time.
- Profit Target: 5% of Initial Simulated Capital. $100k pass at $105,000.
FTMO ACCOUNT (2-Step)
- No profit target. Keep 5% daily + 10% static max loss forever.
- Reward split: up to 90% (base 80% until Scaling Plan or Prime; 1-Step
  starts at 90%). 100% fee refund with first reward withdrawal.
- Standard vs Swing (2-Step only):
  * Evaluation: news trading ALLOWED; weekend/overnight ALLOWED on both.
  * Funded Standard: news restriction on selected high-impact releases
    (independent 2026 reviews: typically 2 minutes before to 2 minutes
    after; do not open/close including SL/TP). Weekend/overnight HOLD
    restricted on Standard funded.
  * Funded Swing: NO news restriction, NO weekend/overnight restriction.
    Lower leverage (typically 1:30 vs 1:100).
- Cannot switch 1-Step to Swing (Swing is not offered on 1-Step).

----- B. CFD 1-STEP CHALLENGE -----
Path: single Evaluation -> KYC + Agreement -> FTMO Account (1-Step).
No Verification phase.
- Profit Target: 10% Initial Simulated Capital, all closed. $100k = $110k.
  NO profit target on subsequent FTMO Account.
- Max Daily Loss: 3% of Initial Simulated Capital. Same midnight CE(S)T
  recalculation vs midnight balance. $100k Day-1 floor $97,000.
  APPLIES on Challenge AND funded 1-Step account.
- Max Loss: END-OF-DAY TRAILING 10%. Recalculated 00:00 CE(S)T as:
  highest midnight balance of any preceding day (or Initial if higher)
  minus 10% of Initial. Limit can only INCREASE, never decrease.
  After a Reward withdrawal + new FTMO Account, the limit RESETS to
  90% of Initial Simulated Capital.
  $100k Day-1 floor $90,000. If midnight bal $104k, next floor $94,000.
  If next midnight $103k, floor STAYS $94,000 (uses high-water $104k).
- Best Day Rule (Challenge AND funded 1-Step): Best Day (highest closed
  P/L day, day starts 00:00 CE(S)T) must be <= 50% of Positive Days'
  Profit (sum of closed profits on profitable days only). Exceeding 50%
  is NOT a hard breach; you must keep trading until Best Day <= 50%.
  Shown in Account MetriX. Example: +10k one day +6k another = 16k
  positive-days; 10/16 = 62.5% -> need Positive Days' Profit >= $20,000
  if $10k remains the best day.
- Circumventing Best Day (hedging, splitting one idea across days) is a
  Forbidden Trading Practice.
- Min trading days: NONE. Time: UNLIMITED.
- Fee: NON-REFUNDABLE (comparison table: Refund = dash).
- Account type: Standard only.
- Stats claimed on 1-Step page: biggest reward $20,765; avg reward
  $3,336.61; shortest pass 2 days.

SECTION 8 — PROHIBITIONS (OFFICIAL FORBIDDEN TRADING PRACTICES)
================================================================================
Quoted substance from https://ftmo.com/en/forbidden-trading-practices/
When performing simulated trading you must NOT:
1. Exploit errors in Services (price display errors, delayed updates,
   external/slow data feed).
2. Manipulative trades alone or with others, including between connected
   accounts / other Program Group operators — e.g. simultaneous opposite
   positions (except opposite positions on a SINGLE simulated account).
3. Trade in conflict with FTMO GTC, FTMO Account T&Cs, or platform T&Cs.
4. Use software, AI, ultra-high-speed tools, or mass data entry that
   manipulates, abuses, or gives unfair advantage.
5. GAP TRADING:
   a) around major global news, macro events, corporate reports/earnings
      that may affect the relevant market; OR
   b) two hours or less before a relevant market is closed for at least
      two hours.
6. Trades that contradict how real markets work, or that FTMO reasonably
   considers may cause financial/reputational harm (overleveraging,
   overexposure, one-sided bets, account rolling). Detail:
   https://ftmo.com/en/blog/gambling-is-not-the-right-way-to-make-money/
7. Hyperactive EAs/robots: > 2,000 server requests per day (opens,
   modifies, closes of trades or pending orders) overloading the server.
8. Strategies that artificially split profit across days without
   distributing risk, to circumvent Best Day Rule (hedging / opposing
   positions on same or highly correlated instruments; partial close of
   the same idea across days).
RISK MANAGEMENT (not reasonably replicable in real markets):
- Substantially larger size vs your other trades (any of your accounts)
- Substantially smaller/larger number of positions vs your other trades
- Repeated activity with higher Risk per Trade Idea / cumulative
  exposure in a symbol or correlated symbols
PERSONAL USE ONLY:
- No third party trading your account; you may not trade someone else's
- Legal entities: only top management / highest executive body may share
Consequences: remove trades from history, restrict platform, disqualify
Evaluation, forfeit Rewards, terminate all agreements.

NEWS / WEEKEND (CFD, official FAQ snippets on challenge pages):
- Evaluation (any type): news OK, weekend/overnight OK.
- Funded Standard: news restriction on selected releases; overnight/
  weekend restricted.
- Funded Swing: no news restriction; overnight/weekend allowed.
- Independent 2026: Standard funded news window often 2 min before/after
  high-impact (NFP, FOMC). Confirm current instrument list in Client Area.
