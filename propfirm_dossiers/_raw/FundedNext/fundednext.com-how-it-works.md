The Prop Firm
## How prop  
trading works
Most skilled traders do not have the capital they need. Most firms with capital do not have a reliable way to find skilled traders without taking direct risk. Prop trading firms exist to close that gap.
A trader pays a one-time fee to enter a challenge. The challenge tests whether they can hit a profit target while staying within defined risk parameters. If they pass, they receive a funded account and keep a share of whatever they earn.
![Bullseye target with a dart hitting the center](https://fundednext.com/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fgetting-started%2Fbulls-eye-dart.png&w=1920&q=75)

What separates one prop firm from another is the rules around that structure: drawdown limits, challenge duration, reward share, tradable assets, platforms, and reward process. Those specifics determine whether a challenge fits the way you trade or works against it.
