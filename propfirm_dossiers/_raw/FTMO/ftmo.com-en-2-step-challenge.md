## Your 2-Step Path Through the Two Phases
### FTMO Challenge
The FTMO Challenge is the first phase. It’s a test of your trading skills with clear, structured rules. Your goal is to show consistent and responsible trading.
![](https://ftmo-frontend-prod.storage.googleapis.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/19160107/ftmo_challenge.webp)
### Verification
Verification is a second phase. You’ll follow the same rules as before, but with a lower Profit Target. This phase is designed to verify consistency.
![](https://ftmo-frontend-prod.storage.googleapis.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/19160108/verification.webp)

After successfully completing the Identity Verification and consenting to the FTMO Account Agreement, you become an FTMO Trader with FTMO Account (2-Step).
