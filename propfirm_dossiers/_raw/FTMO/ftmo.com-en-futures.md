Who is an FTMO Trader, and how do I become one? 
An **FTMO Trader** is an FTMO client who holds a Sim-Funded Account and is eligible to receive monetary payouts.
An **FTMO Live Funded Trader** is an FTMO client who manages a Live Funded Account.
**The journey to becoming an FTMO Trader or an FTMO Live Funded Trader is as follows:**
**Phase 1: Evaluation**
You trade on a simulated futures account and must meet all its Trading Objectives, such as the Profit Target and Consistency Rule, without breaching your Max Drawdown…

How many accounts can I have? 
**Evaluations**
There is no limit on the number of Evaluations you can run at once, and each account runs on its own monthly subscription.
**Sim-Funded Accounts**
You can hold up to **three Sim-Funded Accounts** at the same time, shared across the Growth and Pro products.
There is no maximum total capital allocation across your accounts; the limit is simply the maximum number of Sim-Funded Accounts. Holding more accounts through multiple separate registrations is not permitted.
[ Read more about How many accounts can I have? ](https://ftmo.com/en/futures/faq/ftr-how-many-accounts-can-i-have/)
Who can join FTMO Futures? 
Apart from the exceptions listed below, FTMO Futures is available to clients globally, and we accept registrations from **natural persons** only.
If you are already registered with FTMO as a self-employed person or a legal entity, please contact us at [support@ftmo.com](https://ftmo.com/en/contact/) for more information on the next steps.
No specific qualifications are required. Whether you are a beginner or already know your way around trading and are interested in improving yourself, you're the person we're looking for.
