Is the Premium Programme compatible with the Scaling Plan? 
Yes. The **Premium Programme** and the **Scaling Plan** are two separate and independent programmes. FTMO Traders may participate in both programmes at the same time.
The **Premium Programme** is available to FTMO Traders who qualified through both the **FTMO Challenge: 1-Step** and the **FTMO Challenge: 2-Step**.
The only difference lies in how certain benefits are applied depending on the product. FTMO Traders who qualify through the **FTMO Challenge: 1-Step** start with a **90% simulated profit split from the beginning** …
[ Read more about Is the Premium Programme compatible with the Scaling Plan? ](https://ftmo.com/en/faq/is-the-premium-programme-compatible-with-the-scaling-plan/)
Once I'm in the Premium Programme, do I keep it permanently? 
The Premium Programme is tied to a specific order and applies to FTMO Traders participating in both **FTMO Challenge: 1-Step** and **FTMO Challenge: 2-Step**. The programme is not limited by time.
Compliance with the Premium Programme conditions is assessed across all FTMO Accounts. This means that if you participate in multiple FTMO products (e.g. both 1-Step and 2-Step), any breach of the applicable rules may affect your participation in the Premium Programme as a whole.
**You will be removed…**

Is there anything that would disqualify me from joining the Premium Programme? 
Besides the standard eligibility conditions, you should not have any past or prevailing conflict of interest with FTMO, or engagement in the Forbidden Trading Practices, as laid out in the FTMO Account Agreement (clause 5.9).

Are there any specific account sizes required in order to apply for Prime Status? 
No, all account sizes can be applied for the Prime Status if the conditions are met.
