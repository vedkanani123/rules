## Got Questions?
##  What is Prop Trading?
When you trade the financial markets, you invest your own money, then follow your own rules and win or lose accordingly.
  

Prop Trading companies give you access to their money. You can invest these funds and keep 80% or even 90% of the profits!
  

Of course, the first question that you may ask is, "Why would they give their money to strangers like me?"
  

Before giving access to the actual funds, there must be an evaluation. During this phase, there are some simple rules that you must follow in a Demo environment. This evaluation has a small cost. Once you prove that you can follow these rules, you will progress to the funded stage, where you get to manage the real money!
##  How do I lose an account?
There are **ONLY 4 ways** an account can be lost:
  

**1.)** Violating the **Max Daily Stop Rule**.
  

**2.)** Exceeding the **Max Loss Rule**.
  

**3.)** **Inactivity:** Not opening or closing a trade for a continuous period of **30 days**.
  

**4.)** **Prohibited Practices:** Accounts may be removed from the program if an individual is found to be engaging in activities deemed prohibited.
##  How long does it take to receive my funded account?
Upon passing your Assessment, you will receive an email with instructions.
  

You will need to accept the **"Trader Agreement"** also provide documents and complete our **KYC verification**.
  

Once you are done with both your Funded Account will be created, funded and issued to you typically within **24-48 business hours**.
  

You will receive a confirmation email once this account is being enabled.
##  Do I really get paid during the Evaluation Phases when I trade Futures?
**YES!**
  

Once you complete a Phase and meet the **25% consistency rule** you will be eligible to make a request for that Phase's payout.
##  If I have a breach in my funded account and there are gains on the account, can I withdraw those gains?
**YES!**
  

If you have gains in your funded account at the time of a breach, you will still receive your portion of those gains.
  

For example, if you have **$10,000 in gains** in your funded account after the breach, you would be paid your portion thereof.
