The journey
## From day one to a simulated payout — the whole experience, rehearsed.
Most traders meet prop firm rules for the first time with real pressure on the line. Atmos rebuilds every stage of that journey as an exercise, so the rules are familiar before they matter.
  1. 01
### Choose a simulated account
Pick a simulated balance from $25K to $1M. Nothing is funded, invested or managed — the balance is a scoring baseline for the exercise.
  2. 02
### Phase 1 — Objective
Reach a 10% simulated profit target while respecting a 5% max daily loss and 10% max loss. No minimum trading days, no time limit.
  3. 03
### Phase 2 — Consistency
Repeat the exercise at a 5% target to show the result was process, not luck. Same risk rules, same simulated data feed.
  4. 04
### Simulated payout milestone
Complete both phases and your simulated account moves to the reward-simulation stage, where you practise requesting and tracking a payout cycle end to end.
  5. 05
### Performance certificate
Receive a verifiable certificate summarising your simulated results, risk discipline and drawdown behaviour. Processed within 24h.
  6. 06
### Review and repeat
Break down every simulated trade with analytics on risk, consistency and drawdown, then run the programme again with a sharper process.
