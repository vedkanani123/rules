###  What Is AI Prop and How Does Prop Trading work? 
+
AI Prop is a proprietary trading firm that funds skilled traders to trade in financial markets using the firm's capital. The firm combines traditional prop trading with advanced AI tools to enhance trader performance and risk management. After passing an evaluation challenge, traders receive funding and share profits with AI Prop—allowing them to grow their trading capital without risking personal funds. 
###  How Do I Participate in AI Prop's Funding Challenge? 
+
To join AI Prop's funding challenge, first register and pay the challenge fee. You'll then trade on a simulated account, following specific rules and profit targets within a set timeframe. Once you meet the profit and risk management requirements, you'll pass the challenge and receive a funded trading account. This hands-on evaluation helps us get to know you as a trader, so we can build an amazing partnership together! 
###  What Makes AI Prop a Leading Prop Trading Firm? 
+
AI Prop is licensed and headquartered in Dubai's leading financial center. Our business is committed to transparency, fairness, and trader success. We use blockchain technology to verify all profit payouts, ensuring complete transparency and timely payments. With rapid growth and a thriving community of thousands of successful traders, AI Prop stands out by combining advanced AI tools with a trusted funding process. This commitment to technology, transparent profit sharing, and proven track record makes AI Prop a reliable choice for traders seeking funded accounts. 
###  What Makes AI Prop Different from Other Prop Firms? 
+
AI Prop stands out by integrating advanced AI technology and blockchain for transparency, offering unique AI-powered coaching, trading bots, and analytics tools that help traders improve performance. Compared to traditional prop firms, AI Prop offers flexible funding options, transparent profit-sharing, and a supportive community dedicated to trader development. This tech-driven and trader-focused approach establishes AI Prop as a modern, trustworthy leader in prop trading. 
###  What are the costs involved and what funding account options are available? 
+
To start with AI Prop, traders pay a small challenge fee that covers the evaluation process to qualify for funded accounts. The fee varies depending on the account size and funding level you choose. For example: the fee for $10,000 account is $159. AI Prop offers multiple funding account options tailored to different trader experience levels and capital needs, ranging from smaller starter accounts to large-scale accounts with funding up to several million dollars. This flexible structure allows traders to select the right account that fits their trading style and goals.
