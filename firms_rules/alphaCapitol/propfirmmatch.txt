Alpha Capital Firm AI Summary
Alpha Capital is based in the United Kingdom and was established in November 2021. The firm offers CFD trading through cTrader, MT5, DXTrade, and TradeLocker platforms, with 1-step, 2-step, and 3-step evaluation programs. Account sizes range from $25,000 to $200,000. The 2-step evaluation requires an 8% profit target in Phase 1 and 5% in Phase 2, with a maximum total drawdown of 10% across all accounts. Firm Rules

Inactivity Rule:

All accounts will be deactivated after 30 days of inactivity. To avoid deactivation, log in and maintain an active connection. Once deactivated, accounts cannot be restored.
All or Nothing:

All or nothing practices are prohibited.
Average Trade Duration:

Traders must ensure that the average duration of all their trades exceeds 2minutes. It is required that at least 50% of your requested profit comes from trades that exceed 2 minutes.
Order block spamming:

Entering multiple order in a short period of time is prohibited.
News Trading on Qualified Analyst Accounts:

Allowed only for Swing accounts, However, if a trade is initiated within 2 minutes before or up to 2 minutes after a news release (a 4-minute window), its duration must exceed 2 minutes for the trade to be valid.
For Alpha Direct, Alpha One, and Alpha Pro: Trading around high-impact news is restricted to 5 minutes before or after the news release. News trading is allowed in all evaluation program without any restriction.
Copy Trading:

Allowed, personal accounts only. Proof of ownership is required if Alpha capital account is to be treated as slave. MT5 only
Expert Advisors:

EAs designed solely for trade management are approved for use. Automated EAs that execute trades independently, without human oversight, are strictly prohibited and will not be approved under any circumstances. Expert Advisors (EAs) are allowed for trading on the MT5 Platform, there is currently no EA functionality for cTrader, DX Trade and Tradelocker accounts.
Drawdown Adjustment Rule for Alpha One Accounts: If your X% trailing Max Drawdown resets to the initial balance after reaching a X% profit, maintain a profit buffer to keep trading. Withdrawing all profits will result in account closure.
Account rolling:

(full risk on assessment or qualified accounts to either pass or fail &/or a large quantity of accounts passing & failing within a short timeframe is prohibited. Previous failed accounts are taken into consideration)
Qualified Analyst Allocation Rules:

$300,000 is the MAXIMUM allocation per trading strategy on a Qualified Analyst Account. ""strategy"" is defined as the assets themselves. ​

Additional Information

A full overview of all the challenges offered by Alpha Capital. You'll find up-to-date details for each challenge, including account size, number of steps, profit target, daily and maximum loss limits, profit split, payout frequency, loyalty points, and price. Whether you are new to the firm or comparing different challenge structures, this resource helps you quickly identify the options that fit your trading style, risk tolerance, and goals.

Choosing the right challenge is a critical step toward securing a funded trading account. The structure — from account size and profit target to drawdown limits and payout frequency — will directly influence how you trade and manage risk during the evaluation. A challenge that aligns with your strategy can improve your chances of passing and progressing to the funded stage.

All challenges listed on Prop Firm Match are based on verified information from the firms themselves and are updated regularly to ensure accuracy. Our goal is to give traders a clear, side-by-side view of the available options, making it easier to compare not only across challenges from the same firm but also with those from other top prop firms.

Use the filters on this page to refine your search by asset type, account size, step count, or program type. This allows you to focus on the most relevant challenges and avoid sifting through options that don't fit your needs.
