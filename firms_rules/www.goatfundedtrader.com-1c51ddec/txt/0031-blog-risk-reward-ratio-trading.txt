Subtract your stop-loss from your entry to find risk; subtract your entry from your take-profit to find reward. Divide the reward by the risk for your ratio. A trader entering a long position at $100 with a stop-loss at $95 and a target at $115 risks $5 to pursue $15, producing a 1:3 ratio.
