Their main goal is to generate profits directly from market activities by leveraging the firm's funds. Unlike traditional brokers who earn commissions on client trades, prop firms aim to maximize returns by speculating on price movements in stocks, bonds, currencies, commodities, and derivatives. These firms provide traders with access to capital, technology, and market data, enabling skilled individuals to trade without risking their own money. In exchange, traders share a portion of the profits they make with the firm. This setup helps overcome common barriers for traders, including insufficient capital and a lack of advanced tools. Prop trading can be conducted in physical offices or remotely, allowing global participation. Prop traders employ diverse strategies to exploit market inefficiencies and volatility. These include arbitrage, market making, statistical arbitrage, merger arbitrage, and global macro trading. Risk management is crucial since the firm's funds are at stake. Many prop firms focus on short-term, high-frequency trading to capitalize on quick price fluctuations.Overall, prop trading firms operate by betting on their ability to outperform the market through the use of superior strategies, technology, and risk controls. They generate revenue from the profits earned on their trading activities rather than client fees or commissions.
