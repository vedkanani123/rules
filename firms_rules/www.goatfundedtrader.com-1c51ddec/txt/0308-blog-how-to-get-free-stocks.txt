That structure means fractional portions are bookkeeping entries that rarely transfer cleanly to another broker, so moving a fractional free stock is usually impossible without first selling it into cash.How Do Fractional Shares Behave Differently From Full Shares?Fractional ownership is split across internal accounts rather than represented as isolated certificates, so that voting rights may be limited, and dividends are prorated and credited as cash.
