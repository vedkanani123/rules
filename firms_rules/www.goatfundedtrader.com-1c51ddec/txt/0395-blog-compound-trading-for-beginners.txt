This frequency accelerates momentum because profits start generating additional returns without delay, but it also means setbacks hit harder since your enlarged balance amplifies both gains and losses. Many traders find this pace exhilarating yet demanding, requiring vigilant monitoring and strong emotional control when the market turns against them.Monthly compounding gathers earnings throughout the period before folding them into the account at the cycle's end, creating a more measured rhythm that appeals to those still building consistency. It cushions short-term volatility since your position sizing remains stable across the month, giving you breathing room to refine execution without the pressure of recalculating risk daily. Beginners often start here because it delivers solid cumulative growth while reducing the immediate psychological weight of every single trade outcome.How Frequency Shapes GrowthFrequency determines how quickly your account compounds.
