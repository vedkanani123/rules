Larger capital lets you lower per-trade edge and still reach statistical significance faster, because each trade moves the expectation more than the noise. Set measurable milestones that force upgrades only when your live, order-level metrics match the simulated profile you rely on.How many trades do I need before money matters?When we coached traders in a six-month bootcamp, the pattern became clear: statistical noise eats small samples. Treat capital sizing as an experiment design problem. Simulate thousands of trade-paths with realistic fills, then ask what account size keeps the probability of ruin below a chosen threshold over that sample. That gives you a funding floor tied to confidence, not a guess. Use a simple rule of thumb from those simulations: if your edge per trade is small relative to outcome variance, you need either a much larger sample of trades or more starting capital to reach decision-grade certainty.How do I model slippage, fees, and platform quirks?Operational costs are not incidental; they change which strategies survive. Backtest with fixed per-trade fees, add variable slippage profiles by time of day and order size, and subtract them from expected edge before sizing the account. Track realized slippage for the first 200 live fills as a separate metric, then recompute required capital if slippage or fill delays exceed simulated assumptions. It is exhausting to discover that profitability has evaporated because you treated zero-cost fills as reality; plan for a conservative, stress-tested cost buffer instead.What psychological runway should I budget for?Behavioral survival is numerical. Use your simulated worst-case drawdown over a 95th percentile losing run, then convert that into calendar time you can keep trading without withdrawing. Think in trading days, not percentages; quantify how many consecutive bad weeks you will tolerate before changing rules. That gives you a concrete cushion to choose between adding capital or lowering position size when variance bites.Which performance milestones trigger a capital increase?Define concrete gating metrics before you add capital: a stable realized edge for a specific sample size, a capped maximum drawdown percentage over N trades, and alignment of simulated versus live slippage within a tolerance band.
