Firm Rules

Minimum Trading Days

Requirement: None across all programs
Stop Loss Rule

Requirement: If you fail to place a stop loss at the time of placing an order or executing a trade, the system will automatically close the trade — soft breach

News Trading

Expert Advisor

Allowance: EA's are allowed, except those that are designed for HFT or arbitrage trading
Copy Trading

Allowed

Between Lark funded / Instant funding accounts
Between Lark funded/Instant funding accounts, Other prop firms and personal accounts owned by the same person
Copy trading from an external account (other propfirm or personal accounts) is allowed, but only to a single evaluation account at a time
Copy trading from a Lark funded/Instant funding accounts to a single Lark evaluation account is allowed
Not Allowed

Copy trading between multiple evaluation accounts is not allowed
Copy trading from accounts not owned by the same person is strictly prohibited

For more details on this enhanced functionality, visit PropFirmOne.com
Inactivity

Requirement: Traders must execute at least one trade every 30 days to avoid account inactivity and breach
Gain Limit

Threshold: Daily and/or per-trade gains exceeding $10,000 (calculated at 5 PM EST) trigger the following actions:
Challenge Accounts: One violation results in restarting Phase 1 with no refund but a free evaluation
Simulated Funded Accounts: Gains above $10,000 are excluded from payouts. Three violations lead to account breach with no payout
Trade Calculation: Trades executed at similar times or partially closed count as a single trade
Multi-Phase: Gains made across multiple phases on a single day will be considered toward the daily gain limit
Recommended Risk Limit

Maximum Risk: Maximum risk per simulated trade idea is 1.5%
Trade Idea Definition: A trade idea refers to similar trades, meaning those placed in the same direction on the same instrument, opened and closed within a short interval of 1 to 2 minutes
Margin Limit

Overall Requirement: Overall margin utilization must not exceed the following limits:
Forex: 50%
Metals: 50%
Indices: 75%
Oils: 75%
Crypto: 75%
Stocks: 75%
Weekend Holding | Crypto Trading On Weekends

Allowance: Not allowed except purchased with hold over the weekend add-on
Crypto Breaks: Crypto temporary break between 5-6 pm EST and a 4-hour break from 9 am-1 pm EST on Saturday mornings
All-or-Nothing Trading

Prohibition: This refers to a style of trading where a trader could breach or come close to breaching the drawdown limits in a single trade due to the large volume of positions, lots executed or margin utilization, this practice is prohibited
Holding Into Earnings, Dividends, or Other Corporate Actions

Prohibition: Holding Single Share Equity CFD positions through earnings releases, dividends, or any corporate action is strictly prohibited
Requirement: All such positions must be closed by 3:50 PM ET on the day of the event (or the prior trading day if the event is pre-market)
Violation Consequence: Violation results in an immediate hard breach and any gain or loss on the position will be removed
More on Prohibited Trading Strategies

Prohibited Strategies: All-or-Nothing & Gambling-Based Trading, High-Frequency Trading, Reverse Trading (between different accounts) / Group Hedging, Unauthorized Copy Trading, Holding Into Earnings, Dividends, or Other Corporate Actions

Instant Funding

90% default profit split, On Demand for first payout, then 30 days for subsequent.
Drawdown lock for Instant accounts:

The maximum drawdown will become static and remain fixed at the initial balance after the first withdrawal or once 8% in gains has been achieved.Withdrawing all profit will breach the account
1 Step

Profit Split: 80% default profit split
Payout Frequency: Bi- weekly | weekly with add-on
1 Step monthly based rewards

Traders earn a fixed monthly fee by meeting two conditions within every 30-day period:

Keeping overall drawdown better than -3.5% and recording at least 3 profitable days of 0.5% or more.
Once eligible, the request is submitted via the Dashboard and processed within 2-3 business days.
The reward can be earned indefinitely, but qualification is sequential — missing a month breaks the streak and disqualifies you from the next month as well.
