Congratulations, once the review is a success, you will be a funded Aqua Trader.Account Violations (Hard Breach)All 2 Step Pro Models have Trailing drawdown.Hard beach are serious violations to our established rules. A hard breach happens when a trader exceeds the maximum daily loss or total drawdown limit, resulting in the immediate closure of the account. This applies during evaluation and funded.Daily Drawdown Limit – 5%What does this mean?The maximum daily loss allowed is 5% of your initial account balance. This is calculated daily at 00:00 UTC, based on the higher value of either your account balance or account equity at that time. How is it calculated?At the 00:00 UTC day reset:We check the higher of your account balance or account equity.We subtract 5% of the initial account balance from this value to determine your daily drawdown limit for the next trading day. Examples (Based on a $100,000 Account):$100K Account with $2,000 floating profit at 00:00 UTC:Account equity = $102,000Daily loss limit = $102,000 - $5,000 = $97,000$100K Account with $2,000 floating loss at 00:00 UTC:Account equity = $98,000, but balance = $100,000 (higher value is used)Daily loss limit = $100,000 - $5,000 = $95,000No open trades at 00:00 UTC:Daily drawdown is calculated as 5% of the closed balance.If your balance is $101,000, your limit = $101,000 - $5,000 = $96,000Maximum Total Loss – 10%What is it?A 10% trailing drawdown means your account equity must not fall more than 10% from your highest balance / equity. It includes both realized and unrealised losses and is recalculated as your account value increases.How is it calculated?For a $100,000 account: The trailing drawdown limit is always 10% of starting balance, below your current balance (or equity, depending on how it's defined by your platform).As your balance increases, the drawdown limit moves up. Examples:Account reaches $105,000:Trailing drawdown limit = $105,000 − 10% = $95,000 Account then drops to $103,000: New trailing drawdown limit = $105,000 − 10% = $95,000 Account then rises to $108,000:New trailing drawdown limit = $108,000 − 10% = $98,000 Your account equity must always stay above this 10% trailing limit, which moves with your balance both up and down.Consistency Rule What is it?​The consistency rule is designed to encourage steady and consistent trading. On the Two Step Pro funded stage, your highest profit trading day cannot account for 50% or more of your total profits during a payout period.​Example:​If you make $1,500 in one trading day, your total profits must exceed $3,000 before you are eligible to request a payout.You will not be eligible to request a payout until your highest profit day represents less than 50% of your total profits for the payout period.​What Happens if I Violate the 50% Rule?​Will my account be terminated?​No. Violating the 50% consistency rule will not result in your account being terminated or breached.​You will simply be unable to request a payout until your highest profit trading day falls below 50% of your total profits.​What should I do next?​Continue trading and generating profits until your highest profit day represents less than 50% of your total profits. Once this happens, you will be eligible to request a payout again.​Please note: The 50% consistency rule applies to new Two Step Pro purchases made on or after 31st August 2026. Accounts purchased before this date will remain on the previous 25% consistency rule..Minimum Trading days There are no minimum trading day requirements during the evaluation stages.Once funded, you must complete 3 trading days with at least 0.5% profit on each qualifying day. These days do not need to be consecutive.RewardsProfit SplitTraders receive a 90% profit split as standard.
