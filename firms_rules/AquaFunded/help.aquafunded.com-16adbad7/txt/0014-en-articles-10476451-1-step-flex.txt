Step 1 Trading Objectives:Achieve a 10% profit target on Step 1 without breaking any rules or trading parameters.Adhere to the trading rules and loss limits Upon successfully passing, your entire evaluation period will be reviewed. Congratulations, once the review is a success, you will be a funded Aqua Trader.Account Violations (Hard Breach)All 1 Step Flex accounts use a 3% daily trailing drawdown and a 12% static maximum drawdown. Hard breach are serious violations to our established rules. A hard breach happens when a trader exceeds the maximum daily loss or total drawdown limit, resulting in the immediate closure of the account. This applies during evaluation and funded. Daily Drawdown Limit – 3% What does this mean?The maximum daily loss allowed is 3% of your initial account balance. This is calculated daily at 00:00 UTC, based on the higher value of either your account balance or account equity. How is it calculated?At the 00:00 UTC day reset:We check the higher of your account balance or equity.We subtract 3% of the initial account balance from this value to calculate your daily drawdown limit for the next day.Examples:$100K Account with $2,000 floating profit at 00:00 UTC:Account equity is $102,000.Daily loss limit: $102,000 - $3,000 = $99,000.$100K Account with $2,000 floating loss at 00:00 UTC:Account equity is $98,000, but the balance is $100,000 (higher value used).Daily loss limit: $100,000 - $3,000 = $97,000.No open trades at 00:00 UTC:Daily drawdown is calculated as 3% of the closed balance.Max  Drawdown Limit  – 12% What does this mean?Your maximum drawdown is 12% of your initial account balance. Unlike a trailing drawdown, this drawdown is static, meaning it remains fixed throughout both the evaluation and funded stages regardless of how much profit you make.​​How is it calculated?The maximum drawdown includes all closed trade profits/losses, floating positions, swap fees, and commissions. Examples:$100K AccountYour maximum drawdown is $12,000.Your account balance or equity must never fall below $88,000. $50K Account:Your maximum drawdown is $6,000.Your account balance or equity must never fall below $44,000.Key Notes:Daily drawdown resets daily at 00:00 UTC.Maximum drawdown is static and does not trail as your account grows.All calculations include closed trade profits/losses, floating positions, swap fees, and commissions.Please note: The 12% static maximum drawdown applies to new One Step Flex purchases made on or after 31st August 2026. Accounts purchased before this date will remain on the previous 10% static maximum drawdown rule. The 3% daily trailing drawdown remains unchanged for all accounts.Minimum Trading days (Funded Only)Once you become funded, you must complete a minimum of 3 trading days before requesting your first reward. Each qualifying trading day must achieve at least 0.5% profit. These trading days do not need to be consecutive. Any day with at least 0.5% profit counts towards the 3-day trading period.
