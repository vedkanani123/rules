Keep earning.​​To protect the integrity of our brand and ensure long-term success for all affiliates, we have a few important rules.Please read and follow them carefully — breaking them may result in lost commissions or account removal.​​​What We Encourage:​​Original content that provides real value - engaging, authentic, and informative material that genuinely helps audiences connect with our platform and offerings.​​Using your affiliate link transparently - AquaFunded affiliates should always use their unique referral links transparently, ensuring honesty and clarity in their promotions. Clearly disclosing the use of an affiliate link helps build trust with audiences and fosters genuine engagement with our platform.​​Building a community around trading, funding, or education - AquaFunded affiliates are encouraged to build a strong community centered around trading, funding, and education. By sharing valuable insights, engaging discussions, and helpful resources, our affiliates can build trust, create meaningful connections, and empower others to succeed.​​Staying active and engaged in the affiliate Discord - AquaFunded affiliates are encouraged to stay active and engaged in our affiliate Discord community. By participating in discussions, sharing insights, and networking with fellow affiliates, members can gain valuable strategies, stay informed on the latest updates, and unlock exclusive opportunities to grow their success.​​​What We Don’t Allow:​​No Google Ads or PPC using AquaFunded keywords - AquaFunded strictly prohibits affiliates from using Google Ads or PPC campaigns that target AquaFunded-related keywords. This ensures fair competition and maintains the integrity of organic brand growth within our affiliate network.​​No misleading claims (e.g. fake earnings, unrealistic promises) - Affiliates must avoid making misleading claims, such as exaggerated earnings or unrealistic promises. Transparency and honesty are essential in building trust and credibility within the community.​​No impersonating AquaFunded staff or creating fake profiles - Affiliates must not impersonate AquaFunded staff or create fake profiles. Maintaining authenticity and integrity is crucial to creating a trustworthy and professional environment for all.​​No resale or “black market” accounts - AquaFunded strictly prohibits the resale of accounts. Affiliates must adhere to ethical practices to maintain a fair and transparent network.​​We reward creativity, hustle, and transparency.Play fair, follow the rules, and you’ll keep growing with us long term.​​Additional Questions?
