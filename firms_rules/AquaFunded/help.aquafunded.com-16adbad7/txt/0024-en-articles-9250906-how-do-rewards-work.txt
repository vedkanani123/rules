Updated over 2 weeks agoTable of contentsTraders become eligible to request their first payout 14 days after placing their first trade on the funded account.​After a payout is approved, the next payout becomes available 14 days after the first trade placed after that withdrawal.​To request a payout, the account balance must be above the initial account balance, there must be no rule violations, and all trades and pending orders must be closed.​You can request a payout for any amount you choose, as long as it meets the minimum payout requirement. You do not need to withdraw all available profits.​AquaFunded offers a 24 business hour payout guarantee. If your approved payout is not processed within 24 business hours, we will add an extra $1,000.​The minimum funded account payout is $100.​​Reward via Crypto​Crypto payouts are available for amounts under $5,000.​​Reward via Rise​Payouts of $500 or more can be processed through Rise. Rise allows traders to withdraw by bank transfer or crypto. All AquaFunded payouts are subject to a 2% processing fee. This fee is deducted from the requested payout amount during processing.  Profit Withdrawal Limits for Initial Rewards (100K Accounts and Above)For traders with $100K accounts or larger, the first two reward requests are capped at a maximum withdrawal of $10,000 each. After the first two rewards, this restriction is removed, and there are no further withdrawal limits.​Why Are These Limits Necessary?These limits are designed to support a fair and consistent trading environment while allowing our team to properly evaluate each trader’s approach. The time between purchasing a program and receiving the first rewards is often too short for a complete review of trading patterns. By applying these short-term limits, we promote stability, transparency, and long-term success for every trader in the Aqua community.
