Consistency Rules

Instant Funding Standard

A 20% Daily Consistency Rule applies: You cannot request a payout until the highest profit trading day falls below the 20% of total profits made on that account within that particular period
Instant Funding Pro

A consistency rule of 15% applies, that means that one trading day can not equal or be greater than 15% of the total profits made.
Aquaman (limited account type)

No single trading day can account for 15% or more of your total profits during a payout period.
2 Step Pro Funded accounts:

A 50% Daily Consistency Rule applies: You cannot request a payout until the highest profit trading day falls below the 50% of total profits made on that account within that particular period.
Pay Later Funded Accounts:

A 15% Daily Consistency Rule applies; you cannot request a payout until the highest profit trading day falls below the 25% of total profits made on that account within that particular period.
Firm Rules

Inactivity Rule

All accounts will be deactivated after 30 days of inactivity.
Once deactivated, accounts cannot be restored.
Maximum Loss Per Trade Policy

(Instant Funding, AquaMan and Pay After Pass model): If your floating PnL (profit and loss) reaches or exceeds a loss of -2% (or -1% for the $300,000 and $400,000 accounts) of the account balance, the account will be permanently closed.
News Trading

News trading is allowed. Profits on trades opened or closed 5 minutes before/after high-impact news is capped at 0.5% per cycle.
News trading is allowed in all evaluation program without any restriction.
Wave stop

Wave Stop applies to Funded Accounts only.
If your open trades reach a 2% loss of your account balance, Wave Stop will, in most cases, automatically close all open trades across all symbols.
This is a soft breach, meaning you can continue trading immediately without further restrictions.
All or Nothing

All or nothing practices are prohibited.
Margin Limit

Using more than 80% of available margin on a single trade is prohibited.

Excessive risk taking in terms of the % of an account risked in a single simulated-trade.

Excessive cumulative risk taking in terms of the % of an account risked in a series of overlapping simulated-trades.

Choice of strategy

Traders are not allowed to use one strategy to pass an assessment and then switch to a different strategy in a funded account.
Copy Trading

Allowed between personal accounts only.
The following copy trading is allowed:
Copy trading between AquaFunded accounts.
Copy trading from Funded to Evaluation accounts and vice versa.
Copy trading between AquaFunded account and external accounts.

Additional Information

A full overview of all the challenges offered by AquaFunded. You'll find up-to-date details for each challenge, including account size, number of steps, profit target, daily and maximum loss limits, profit split, payout frequency, loyalty points, and price. Whether you are new to the firm or comparing different challenge structures, this resource helps you quickly identify the options that fit your trading style, risk tolerance, and goals.

Choosing the right challenge is a critical step toward securing a funded trading account. The structure — from account size and profit target to drawdown limits and payout frequency — will directly influence how you trade and manage risk during the evaluation. A challenge that aligns with your strategy can improve your chances of passing and progressing to the funded stage.

All challenges listed on Prop Firm Match are based on verified information from the firms themselves and are updated regularly to ensure accuracy. Our goal is to give traders a clear, side-by-side view of the available options, making it easier to compare not only across challenges from the same firm but also with those from other top prop firms.

Use the filters on this page to refine your search by asset type, account size, step count, or program type. This allows you to focus on the most relevant challenges and avoid sifting through options that don't fit your needs.
