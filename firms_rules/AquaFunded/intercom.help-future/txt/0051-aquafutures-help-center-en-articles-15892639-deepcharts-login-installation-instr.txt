Step 1: Purchase Your Account and Receive Credentials After purchasing your account, you will receive a credentials email.This email includes:Your usernameYour passwordA platform login link (highlighted in the image)Important notes:If you do not receive the credentials email, contact the support team.If your credentials say “Same as previous account” and you no longer have your old login details, contact the support team.  Step 2: Log In to the PlatformClick the platform link in the credentials email.Use the username and password from the email to log in.   Step 3: Sign the dxFeed Agreement If this is your first AquaFutures account using dxFeed, you must sign the dxFeed agreement.The agreement prompt is located in the top-right area of the dashboard (highlighted in the image).  Step 4: Choose Your Subscriber Status To complete the dxFeed agreement, you must select your subscriber status.Use the Market Data Self-Certification link (highlighted in the image) to determine which option applies to you. Important note:If you select the wrong status or experience issues after submitting, contact the support team.  Step 5: Choose Your Trading Platform After completing the subscription certification, you can choose your trading platform.You will see two areas:Left side options:DeepChart WebDownload Platform (DeepChart Desktop or DeepDOM Desktop)Right side:Direct platform download linkNote:DeepChart Web does not require a separate installation and is often easier for quick access.  Step 6a: Using DeepChart WebSelect DeepChart Web to open the browser-based platform.When the platform opens:Review and accept the user and platform agreementsYour charts will load automaticallyAt the top of the platform, confirm the data feed is set to Aqua FuturesA green dot confirms you are connectedYou can now begin trading.If the data feed does not connect, contact the support team.  Step 6b: Downloading the DeepChart Desktop App Select DeepChart Desktop (shown in image above)You will be taken to a page with:A download linkA license key (highlighted in image below)Download the platform and follow the installation instructions on your device  Step 7: Log In to the DeepChart Desktop App When opening the desktop app, you will be asked to select a feed connection.Choose:Name: AquaFuturesFeed Source: dxFeed Prop​(Highlighted in the image)After selecting the feed:Enter the same username and password sent in your credentials emailThese are the same credentials used for the dxFeed/Volumetrica dashboard  Step 8: Set Up Your Trading WorkspaceAfter logging in:Confirm the AquaFutures data feed is selected at the topOnce connected, use the “New” dropdown menu to open:ChartsOrder entry windowsAny other tools you needYou are now ready to trade.Related ArticlesAquaFutures' Platform OverviewHow to trade on DeepchartsDeepcharts FAQGetting started with DeepchartsWhy Are My Trades Being Rejected?Did this answer your question?😞😐😃Table of contentsTrade with our Capital and keep 100% of the ProfitWe run on Fin
