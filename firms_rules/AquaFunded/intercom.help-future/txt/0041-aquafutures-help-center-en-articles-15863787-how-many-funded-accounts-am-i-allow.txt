July 7, 2026Traders are allowed to maintain up to 3 active funded accounts at a time. If you choose to pursue additional accounts beyond this limit, you will need to continue paying the monthly subscription fee for each additional evaluation account. This limit applies to Instant Funded accounts as well. Once an evaluation account successfully meets the profit target and passes the evaluation phase, the subscription fee will no longer be required. If you already have 3 active funded accounts, your newly passed account will be placed on hold until one of your existing funded accounts is breached. While the account is on hold, no further subscription payments are required. If you have any questions about our evaluation plans, please feel free to contact our support team. They are available 24/7 to assist you.
