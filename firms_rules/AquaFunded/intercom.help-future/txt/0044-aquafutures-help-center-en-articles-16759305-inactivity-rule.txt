Skip to main contentAccount DashboardAccount DashboardSearch for articles...All CollectionsTrading Rules and GuidelinesInactivity RuleInactivity Rule Updated over a week agoTable of contentsTo keep all AquaFunded Futures accounts active and properly maintained, traders must remain active on their accounts.​How Does the 7-Day Inactivity Rule Work?If no trade is executed on your account for 7 consecutive calendar days, the account will be considered inactive and will automatically closed.The 7-day period begins from the date of your last executed trade.​How Do I Keep My Account Active?Simply execute at least one trade within every 7-day period.There is no minimum profit requirement for this rule. The purpose is only to ensure that the account remains actively used.​Does This Apply to Weekends?Yes. The inactivity period is calculated using calendar days, meaning weekends are included in the 7-day period.​What Happens If My Account Is Closed Due to Inactivity?Accounts closed due to the 7-Day Inactivity Rule cannot be reinstated or reset.
