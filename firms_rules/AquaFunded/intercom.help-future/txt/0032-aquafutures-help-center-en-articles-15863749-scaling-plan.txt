This is not to be confused with contracts per individual position. For example: a trader with a brand new $50,000 Beginner account opens a position with 7 micro contracts. They are eligible to open 10 micro contracts (or 1 mini contract) in total. This means the trader will be able to open 3 more micro contracts while the initial trade of 7 micro contracts is open.  The scaling plan will pertain to the account's balance at the EOD. For example: a trader with a brand new $50,000 Beginner account records $1,250 in profits during their first trading day. The trader's account position sizes will not be scalable until their profits have been recorded at the time of daily market close (4:10pm EST). Upon the next market open, the scaling rule will be applicable to the account.
