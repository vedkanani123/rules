July 7, 2026Table of contentsAny evaluation account that reaches the profit target is guaranteed to be fully reviewed within 24 business hours.You will receive a verdict of Pass, Fail, or Reset during that time. This is not a guarantee of passing. It’s a promise that you’ll know where you stand quickly. No more waiting days to find out what’s next!  What You’ll ReceiveOnce you reach the profit target, our team will:Review your full account trading historyDetermine if the account qualifies to pass based on profit and rule complianceSend you an email within 24 business hours with your verdict and next stepsIf your account passes, we will:Automatically send you a KYC request (or skip this step if already completed)Issue your funded account as soon as KYC is approved When Does the 24-Hour Clock Start?Our business hours occur on Monday-Friday at 8am-4pm Central daily. The 24-hour review guarantee is based on these hours. This means the timer pauses when business hours are closed and resumes once they reopen. However, our team does occasionally continue to conduct reviews over the weekend. If you reach your profit target close to market close on Friday, that does not mean you’ll always have to wait until the beginning of next week to receive your review. In many cases, weekend reviews are completed ahead of time, even though the guarantee only applies to business hours. So while the 24 business hour guarantee ensures a maximum window, most accounts are reviewed much sooner, often within just a few hours. Example:- You reach your profit target at 2:00 PM CT on Monday - Your account will be reviewed and you will receive a verdict by 2:00 PM CT on Thursday at the latest Additional DetailsThis guarantee applies strictly to the account review stage and does not include the time required for KYC. Once an account is reviewed and passed, the KYC process begins separately. Resets or failures will only apply to accounts that violated rules or failed to meet evaluation conditions, such as the consistency requirement. While the 24 business hour window is our official guarantee, most accounts are reviewed much sooner. In nearly all cases, traders receive their verdict well before the maximum timeframe.  Why We Do ItWe believe that if you’ve done the work to pass your evaluation, you deserve timely communication and next steps.We’ve heard from traders across the industry who’ve waited days or even weeks for decisions. We think that’s unacceptable.At AquaFutures, we prioritize your progress.
