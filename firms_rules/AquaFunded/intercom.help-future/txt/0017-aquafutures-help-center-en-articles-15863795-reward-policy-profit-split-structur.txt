growth.Profit Split StructureBeginner Accounts, Instant Standard Accounts & Instant Pro AccountsAll approved withdrawals are paid at a 100% profit split until a trader reaches $15,000 in total lifetime approved withdrawals.Once a trader exceeds $15,000 in lifetime withdrawals, all future withdrawals are paid at a 90% profit split.If a withdrawal request crosses the $15,000 threshold, the profit split will be calculated proportionally.ExampleA trader has already received $14,000 in approved withdrawals and submits a withdrawal request for $1,500.The first $1,000 is paid at a 100% profit split.The remaining $500 is paid at a 90% profit split.The lifetime withdrawal threshold applies across all accounts, profiles, and email addresses associated with the same trader.Standard AccountsStandard Accounts operate under a fixed 90% profit split from the first withdrawal.The $15,000 lifetime withdrawal threshold does not apply to Standard Accounts.All approved withdrawals are processed at a 90% profit split regardless of withdrawal history.Reward CapsBeginner Accounts, Instant Standard Accounts & Instant Pro AccountsAccount SizeMaximum Withdrawal Per
