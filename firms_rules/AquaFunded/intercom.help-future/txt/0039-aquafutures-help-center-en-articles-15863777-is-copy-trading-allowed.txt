July 7, 2026Yes, copy trading is allowed on AquaFutures only between accounts that are fully owned and operated by the same individual. Traders may use trade copier tools to mirror trades between their own AquaFutures accounts or between AquaFutures accounts and personal external accounts they personally own and manage.
