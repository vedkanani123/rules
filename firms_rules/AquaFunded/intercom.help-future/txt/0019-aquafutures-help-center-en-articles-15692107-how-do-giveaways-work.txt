Discover how our giveaways work and how you can participate in AquaFutures promotions AquaFutures provides exclusive giveaways as a way to offer members access to trading opportunities and unique rewards. These giveaways are open to both free and paid members. Please read the details below to understand how to participate and what to expect. Giveaway Access PeriodGiveaway accounts typically provide 30 days of free access for trading and completing the challenge. During this period, winners have full access to the account to trade, meet targets, and complete the challenge requirements. For example: If a giveaway account is awarded to you, you will have 30 days to use it without any charges. This period is designed to allow ample time to complete the challenge and meet account objectives. Restricted TerritoriesGiveaway claims from restricted territories will not be eligible to receive their account. It is the responsibility of the giveaway host and giveaway participants to be knowledgeable of the restrictions and criteria to be eligible to claim an AquaFutures account. If found to be a resident or citizen of a restricted territory, the account claim will need to be issued to another random participant of the giveaway that meets all necessary criteria. See our restricted territories policy here. Ongoing Access RequirementsIf the challenge is not successfully completed within the free 30-day period, a monthly access fee will be charged for continued use in subsequent months. This fee ensures ongoing access until the challenge is completed or the account is closed. To claim a giveaway account, a payment method must be submitted at the time of claim in the event the monthly payment is applied after the 30 days. For example: If the 30-day period expires without completion of the challenge, you will be billed the monthly fee starting from the following month to maintain access to the account. Activation Fee Upon Passing the ChallengeJust as with our regular Beginner and Standard accounts, there is no activation fee for giveaway accounts. Once you reach the profit target on your evaluation, your account history will be reviewed, and if found to meet the necessary criteria and trading rules, you account will be upgraded to a funded account with no activation fee necessary. Participation Rules and Entry ProcessCheck Eligibility: Verify that you meet all entry requirements for the specific giveaway, such as membership status.Follow Entry Instructions: Each giveaway will have clear steps for entry, which could include engaging with community content, meeting activity requirements, or other instructions.Winner Selection and Claiming PrizesWinners are selected randomly (unless otherwise specified) and will be announced via AquaFutures' community channels. Once announced, winners have a limited time to claim their prize. Failing to respond within this timeframe may result in forfeiting the prize to an alternate winner. To claim a giveaway account, open a support ticket or ask for assistance in our community Discord to complete the claims process. Can I win more than one giveaway?Giveaways are limited to 1 per customer per 30 days. What happens if I don’t claim my prize in time?​Answer: Unclaimed prizes will be reassigned to ensure all giveaway benefits are distributed. If you have questions, reach out to our support team anytime.
