The Chart helps you analyze market movement, while the DOM allows you to place, modify, and manage orders quickly.This guide explains how to set up and use both tools across desktop, web, and mobile platforms.What is the DOM?The DOM (Depth of Market) displays real-time bid and ask prices along with available market liquidity.Using the DOM, you can:Place Buy and Sell ordersEnter Limit and Stop ordersMonitor market activityManage open positionsAdjust or cancel orders quicklyMany active futures traders use the DOM for faster execution and order management.Setting Up the DOM and Chart on DesktopStep 1: Log In to TradovateOpen the Tradovate desktop application and sign in to your account.Step 2: Open the ChartAdd a Chart window to your workspace.Step 3: Open the DOMAdd a DOM (Depth of Market) window.Step 4: Select Your InstrumentChoose the same futures contract on both the Chart and DOM.Examples include:NQMNQESMESCLStep 5: Arrange Your WorkspacePlace the Chart and DOM side by side for easier monitoring and execution.Trading with the DOM and Chart on DesktopUse the Chart for AnalysisThe Chart allows you to:Identify trendsMark support and resistance levelsReview price actionPlan potential trade entries and exitsUse the DOM for ExecutionThe DOM allows you to:Buy and sell directly from the price ladderPlace Market OrdersPlace Limit OrdersPlace Stop OrdersManage open positions in real timeManage Existing TradesOnce a trade is active, you can:Modify ordersAdjust Stop Loss levelsAdjust Take Profit levelsClose positions directly from the DOM or ChartUsing the DOM and Chart on the Web PlatformStep 1: Log InAccess the Tradovate web platform and sign in.Step 2: Open Both PanelsAdd a Chart panel and a DOM panel to your workspace.Step 3: Select the Same InstrumentEnsure both windows are displaying the same futures contract.Step 4: Analyze and ExecuteUse the Chart to review market conditions and the DOM to place and manage your trades.Using the DOM and Chart on the Mobile AppStep 1: Open the Tradovate AppLaunch the mobile application and log in.Step 2: Select an InstrumentChoose the futures contract you want to trade.Step 3: Use Chart TradingFrom the Chart, you can:View price movementAnalyze market structurePlace orders directly from supported chart functionsStep 4: Access the DOMOpen the Depth of Market view to:Monitor order flowPlace Buy and Sell ordersManage active tradesReview current market pricesStep 5: Monitor Your PositionTrack:Entry priceStop LossTake ProfitOpen profit and lossActive ordersdirectly from the mobile application.Tips for Using the DOM and Chart TogetherFor the best trading experience:Use the Chart to identify trade opportunities.Use the DOM for faster order execution.Always verify your contract size before placing orders.Confirm your Stop Loss and Take Profit levels before entering a trade.Monitor active positions closely during periods of high volatility.Need Help?If you have any questions about using the DOM, Chart Trading, or placing orders in Tradovate, the AquaFutures Support Team is available to assist you.
