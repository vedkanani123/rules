The total fees column can be used in reference to how many contracts you are opening on each instrument. Commissions are applied to every trade between the virtual evaluation and virtual funded stage of your account. For example: opening 2 contracts of /MNQ will require a commission of $1.48 to be deducted from your virtual balance. ($0.74 x 2 = $1.48) Another example: opening 1 contract of /ES will require a commission of $2.80 to be deducted from your virtual balance.
