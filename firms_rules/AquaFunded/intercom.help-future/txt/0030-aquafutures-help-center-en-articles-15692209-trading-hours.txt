Skip to main contentAccount DashboardAccount DashboardSearch for articles...All CollectionsTrading Rules and GuidelinesTrading HoursTrading Hours June 29, 2026Table of contentsThe market is regularly open from Sunday 10pm (UTC) to Friday 8:10pm (UTC), unless there are specific holiday hours during the week.​All open trades will automatically close when the market closes.This automatic closure does not count as a rule violation and will not result in an account breach.Trading will resume when the market opens the following day. Day-by-Day Trading Hours (UTC)Day 1Market opens at 10pm UTC Sunday, and closes at 8:10pm UTC Monday.Day 2Market opens at 10pm UTC Monday, and closes at 8:10pm UTC Tuesday.Day 3Market opens at 10pm UTC Tuesday, and closes at 8:10pm UTC Wednesday.Day 4Market opens at 10pm UTC Wednesday., and closes at 8:10pm UTC Thursday.Day 5Market opens at 10pm UTC Thursday, and closes at 8:10pm UTC Friday.
