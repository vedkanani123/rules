Consistency Rules

Instant Funding:

Your best trading day must not account for more than 15% of your total profit. (20% consistency rule option is available for a higher price)
Firm Rules

News Trading

News trading is not allowed 5 minutes before and after high impact news. Trades that were opened at least two hours prior to the scheduled high-impact news event are allowed to close during the restricted period, including closure triggered by Take Profit or Stop Loss.
News trading is allowed on the Instant Funding Pro without restriction.

4%
Max Floating Loss

2-Step Funded Accounts (5% Daily Loss option) and Instant Pro: 3% of the account size in floating loss on the same symbol
2-Step Funded Accounts (4% Daily Loss option), 1-Step Funded Accounts, Instant Funding, and Phoenix: 2% of the account size in floating loss on the same symbol
Prohibited Practices:

Risk management

Allocating a substantial or majority portion of the allowable daily loss or available margin to a single trade idea, whether through realized losses, stop-loss exposure, or a set of highly correlated positions, is prohibited.
Martingale

Martingale is a trading strategy that involves increasing position size after a loss in an attempt to recover previous losses with a single winning trade; the use of martingale strategies is prohibited.
Grid Trading/ Hedging: Grid Trading or Hedging is a trading approach where opposing buy and sell orders for the same instrument are strategically placed to achieve equivalent or similar risk and is prohibited.
Phoenix Scaling Program

The Phoenix Program provides a unique opportunity for rewarding traders who consistently exhibit expert performance. Account holders will be automatically progressed to the next phase upon completion of the below detailed Targets for Scaling.

Traders may begin at Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, or Level 4 of the program.

Once the 10% profit target for the selected starting level is achieved, that 10% will be converted into withdrawable credit at the next level.

The credit may be withdrawn at the next payout, provided the 14-day minimum payout interval and the requirement of 3 minimum profitable days are met.

For example, a trader starting at Level 2 ($5,000 starting balance) who reaches the 10% profit target ($500) will automatically scale to Level 3 ($10,000 starting balance).

The $500 profit is converted into withdrawable credit.
At this point, the trader has two options:

Option 1 – Continue scaling

The trader may continue trading and work toward the Level 3 profit target of 10 ($1,000). Once this target is reached, the account will scale to the next level.
Option 2 – Request a payout

After the minimum 14-day trading period from the first trade and at least 3 profitable days are met, the trader may request a payout. Any new profits generated on the Level 3 account can be withdrawn together with the $500 credit.

For example, if the trader earns an additional $200 on the Level 3 account, they may request a payout of $700 ($500 credit + $200 profit), provided the payout conditions are satisfied.

If the trader chooses to withdraw the $700, they will still need to generate the full Level 3 profit target of 10% ($1,000) in order to scale up to the next level.

The Sprint Challenge

We're introducing a new challenge type: The Sprint Challenge, a fast-paced, time-based trading experience designed to deliver quick results and instant rewards. Traders aim to hit a small profit target within a strict time window and receive direct payouts with a 100% profit split.

Key Highlights:-
• Short-duration challenges (1h to 8h) with clear, achievable profit targets.
• Instant payout model – earn and withdraw profits immediately after success
• Simple rule structure:  Profit Target + Max Loss + Time Limit = Fixed Payout
• High reward potential with multipliers (2x–5x)

How It Works:-

Choose your account size, timeframe, and multiplier
The challenge begins as soon as the first trade is placed
Hit the profit target within the time limit without breaching max loss
Request payout instantly upon success

What Makes It Unique:-

Direct payout model (not traditional scaling or evaluation phases)
No complex trading restrictions (except news, gap, and market open trading limits)
One successful trade setup can pass the challenge.

Designed for speed, simplicity, and high engagement
