CFDsFuturesLabsRewardsMoreENLoginCFDsFuturesLabsRewardsSearch forex termsCFDs GlossaryUnrealized P&LUnrealized P&LWhat is Unrealized P&L (Profit and Loss) in Trading?Unrealized P&L, also written as unrealized profit and loss or unrealized pnl, is the running profit or loss on your open CFD positions. It’s “unrealized” because you haven’t closed the trade yet. Once you close it (fully or partially), it becomes realized P&L.Unrealized Profit and Loss:Shows how your open trades are doing right now.Affects your equity and available margin, even though it’s not locked in.Can change every second as prices move.How to Calculate Unrealized P&L?For most CFDs, platforms calculate it like this (before fees):Long (Buy): (Current Price − Entry Price) × Position Size × Point ValueShort (Sell): (Entry Price − Current Price) × Position Size × Point ValueThen the platform applies spread, commission, and overnight funding (if any).Unrealized P&L ExampleYou buy EURUSD at 1.10000. Later, price is 1.10201.Price moved 0.00201 (that’s 20.1 pips if 1 pip = 0.00010).If your position size makes each pip worth 10 Dollars, your unrealized profit is 20.1 × 10 = 201 Dollars (before costs).If price falls below your entry, your unrealized profit and loss or Floating P&L, turns negative (an unrealized loss).(You’ll see similar logic on indices, commodities, or stocks; only the “point value” per contract differs.)When Unrealized Turns RealizedClose the trade: your current unrealized number is locked in as realized P&L.Partial close: part becomes realized; the remainder stays unrealized on the reduced position.Where You’ll See It in Your PlatformNext to each open position.In the account summary as “Unrealized P&L” or “Floating P&L”.Common Mistakes to AvoidCounting it as final: it can reverse until you close the trade.Ignoring costs: spread, commissions, and overnight funding can reduce your P&L.Confusing equity and balance: balance excludes unrealized p&l; equity includes it.Other Glossary TermsUUptrendAn uptrend is when a market’s price consistently moves higher over time, forming higher highs and higher lows, showing strong buyer control and overall upward momentum.Unemployment RateThe unemployment rate measures the percentage of people in the labor force who are jobless but actively seeking work, indicating how strong or weak a country’s job market is.UnitA unit in CFDs is the smallest tradable size that shows how much of an asset you control; more units increase exposure and risk, while fewer units offer safer control.USD IndexThe USD Index (USDX) measures the US Dollar’s strength against major global currencies like the Euro, Yen, and Pound, showing whether the dollar is gaining or losing value overall.Start yourFundedNext challengeThousands of traders are already getting rewarded by FundedNext.
