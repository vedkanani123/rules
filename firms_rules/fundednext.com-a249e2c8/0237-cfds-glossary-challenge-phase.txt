Instead of risking your own money, you prove your ability by following the rules and reaching a profit target set by the firm.At FundedNext, this step is called the FundedNext Challenge Phase. It’s designed to check if you can trade responsibly, manage risk, and stay consistent. Once you pass, you move forward to either a verification stage (in a 2-phase challenge) or directly to a funded account (in a 1-phase funded challenge).How does the Challenge Phase work?2-Phase Challenge: In this model, you first complete Phase 1 Challenge with a set profit target. If successful, you move to Phase 2, where you prove consistency under similar or slightly easier rules. After passing both, you qualify for a funded account.1-Phase Challenge: Some firms, including FundedNext, also offer a 1 Phase Challenge option. Here, you only need to pass one stage instead of two, which makes the process faster. This is often referred to as the one phase funded challenge.Challenge Phase ExampleImagine you join a FundedNext Challenge Phase account with a profit target of 10% of your initial balance. If you successfully reach this profit without breaking any rules or breaching the account, you pass Phase 1:In a 2-phase challenge, you would then need to complete Phase 2 to fully qualify for the funded account.In a 1-phase challenge, passing the first stage means you can directly start trading a funded account.Other Glossary TermsCCFD (Contract for Difference)A CFD is a financial agreement that allows you to speculate on the price movement of assets, such as stocks, currencies, indices, cryptos, or commodities, without owning them.Currency PairA currency pair in trading shows the price of one currency compared to another.Cross-Currency PairA cross currency pair is any currency pair that does not include the U.S. Dollar (USD).Close PriceClosing price (or close price) is the last price of a CFDs pair when a trading period ends.CommissionCommission (or forex commission, forex trading commission) is like a small service charge you pay to the forex broker every time you open or close a trade.Show MoreStart yourFundedNext challengeThousands of traders are already getting rewarded by FundedNext.
