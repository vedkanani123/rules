CFDsFuturesLabsRewardsMoreENLoginCFDsFuturesLabsRewardsSearch forex termsCFDs GlossaryNew York SessionNew York SessionWhat Is the New York Session?The New York Session is one of the busiest and most important trading sessions in the world. It takes place when financial markets in New York are open, and during this time trading activity is usually very high. Many traders prefer this session because the US Dollar is the most traded currency globally, and major US economic news is often released during these hours. This can lead to strong price movements, increased volatility compared to other sessions, and more trading opportunities.New York Session Time (GMT)New York Session time (GMT): 1:00 PM to 10:00 PM GMTThis period also overlaps with the London Session, which makes it one of the most active times of the trading day.Why Is the New York Session Important?The New York trading session time is important because:It often includes major US news releases like Non-Farm Payrolls, interest rate decisions, and inflation data.The US Dollar plays a key role in global trade, so its movements impact many markets.The overlap with the London Session creates strong price movements, with higher volatility resulting in higher trading activity.Liquidity is very high, meaning trades can be executed quickly, but price changes can also be fast and unpredictable.The fast price changes and high trading activity make this session especially appealing for day traders and scalpers who want to earn from quick, big moves.Pairs to Look out for During the NY SessionDuring the New York Session, some currency pairs become especially active. These include:EURUSD – often sees sharp moves when US and European markets react to news.GBPUSD – the British Pound and Dollar pair is very active.USDJPY – can move strongly when US events affect the Dollar.USDCAD – often influenced by both US and Canadian economic data released during this time.Other Glossary TermsNNews TradingNews trading is a strategy where traders make quick decisions based on major economic or political news that can cause sharp price movements in currencies, stocks, or commodities.NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls)The NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls) report is a monthly US jobs report showing how many positions were added or lost, excluding farm, government, and private household workers.Net PositionNet position shows the difference between total buy and sell trades, indicating whether a trader holds more long (buy) or short (sell) positions in a given asset.Notional ValueNotional value is the total value of a trading position, showing the full market exposure you control, not just the smaller margin used to open the trade.Start yourFundedNext challengeThousands of traders are already getting rewarded by FundedNext.
