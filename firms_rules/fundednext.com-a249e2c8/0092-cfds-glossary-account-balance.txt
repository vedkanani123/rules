CFDsFuturesLabsRewardsMoreENLoginCFDsFuturesLabsRewardsSearch forex termsCFDs GlossaryAccount BalanceAccount BalanceWhat is account balance in CFDs trading?In CFDs (Contract for Difference) trading, account balance refers to the amount of funds currently available in a trader’s account, excluding any unrealized gains or losses from running positions. It serves as the financial foundation for trading activities, representing the capital that traders can use to place new trades or meet margin requirements.What Is the Current Account Balance vs. Equity?The current account balance shows the total funds in a trader’s account based on closed positions, along with any deposits or withdrawals. It does not reflect gains or losses from running trades. The balance only changes once an active trade is closed and the results are finalized.Equity represents the live value of a trader’s account. It includes the current account balance plus or minus any floating profit or loss from open positions. Since running trades change in value with market movements, equity fluctuates constantly in real time.For example: A Forex trader deposits $10,000 and closes a EURUSD trade with a $600 gain; the account balance updates to $10,600. If there's a running GBPUSD position currently floating a $400 loss, the equity will reflect $10,200, while the account balance remains $10,600.This distinction is essential for managing margin levels, understanding drawdowns, and preventing stop-outs.Why Account Balance Matters in CFDs and Forex TradingMonitoring account balance is critical for every Forex or CFD trader. It helps traders:Meet platform margin requirementsManage risk and drawdownsTrack performance over timeMake informed decisions on scaling or reducing position sizesStaying aware of one’s account balance supports disciplined trading, effective capital management, and long-term sustainability in both Forex and CFDs environments.Other Glossary TermsAAsset ClassAn asset class is a group of similar financial instruments you can trade, such as forex, commodities, indices, cryptocurrencies, stocks, or options.Ask PriceIn CFDs (Contract for Difference) trading, the ask price is the price at which traders can buy an asset.ArbitrageArbitrage trading involves buying an asset at a lower price on one platform and selling it almost simultaneously at a higher price on another.Automated TradingAutomated trading, also called auto trading or algorithmic trading is the process of executing trades based on pre-programmed rules and conditions.Asian SessionThe Asian trading session runs approximately from 2:00 AM to 11:00 AM (GMT+3).Start yourFundedNext challengeThousands of traders are already getting rewarded by FundedNext.
