How the 12% Static Maximum Loss Limit Works
The Maximum Loss Limit on FNL:002 is 12%, and the drawdown type is static. FundedNext applies the same static MLL and DLL structure here as on its other CFD challenges.
At 12% of a $25,000 account, the limit is $3,000, placing the floor at $22,000. The Daily Loss Limit is 4%, or $1,000, so three consecutive days at the full daily limit would reach the maximum loss limit exactly.
What Counts as a Minimum Profitable Day
For a $25,000 account, a profitable day requires $250 in profit, which is 1% of the account. Profit is attributed to the day the trade closes, regardless of when it was opened.
