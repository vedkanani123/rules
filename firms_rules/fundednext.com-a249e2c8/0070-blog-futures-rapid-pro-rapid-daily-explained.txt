Where they differ is how you get paid and how daily risk is controlled. You select Rapid Pro or Rapid Daily at checkout, and the path cannot be changed afterward.

How the Futures Rapid Challenge works: the fastest path to a FundedNext Account
The structure is deliberately simple: one evaluation phase, one profit target, and a direct route to the FundedNext Account once both are met.
One phase, one target
Futures Rapid is a single-phase evaluation. There is no other step: reach the profit target for your account size while staying above the EOD trailing drawdown floor, and the challenge is complete. There are no minimum trading days and no Benchmark Days.
What “pass in one day” requires in practice
On a 50K account, a 1-day pass means closing the session $3,000 up without your balance or equity touching the $48,000 floor. On Rapid Daily, the $1,000 daily loss limit also applies on a 50K account, so the same target must be hit with tighter intraday risk. There are no daily loss limit in the Rapid Pro accounts, maintaining the maximum loss limit will suffice. Restricted strategies remain prohibited throughout.
After the pass
Your account transitions to a FundedNext Account with no activation fee, where the path you chose at purchase defines the reward cadence and rules that follow.
Futures Rapid Pro vs. Rapid Daily: which one should you choose?
Choose Futures Rapid Pro if you want maximum intraday flexibility. There is no daily loss limit by default, and rewards can be withdrawn every 3 days. The trade-off is a 40% consistency rule in the FundedNext Account: your best single day cannot exceed 40% of total profit before a reward becomes eligible. There is an optional add-on for a daily loss limit, which will make the fee cheaper.
Choose Futures Rapid Daily if you want the fastest reward cycle and simpler rules. You can request rewards daily with no consistency rule at any stage. The trade-offs are a daily loss limit throughout the journey and a buffer rule: your EOD balance must reach starting balance + maximum loss limit + $100 ($52,100 on a 50K account) before profits above that level become withdrawable.
How the 90% Reward Share works
Both paths pay 90% of each approved Performance Reward. Eligibility requires at least $500 profit in the current cycle, with withdrawals between $250 and a size-based cap of $800 (25K), $1,200 (50K), or $2,500 (100K) per cycle. There are no minimum trading days. The FundedNext Account concludes after the 5th Performance Reward.
Example 1 (50K Rapid Daily): you finish the day at $53,000, which is $900 above the $52,100 buffer. That $900 is withdrawable, and at a 90% share you keep $810.
Example 2 (50K Rapid Pro): over a 3-day reward window you close at $51,000, which is $1,000 of profit for the cycle. There is no buffer rule on Pro, so that profit sits above your $50,000 starting balance. Your best single day was $350, which is 35% of the total and under the 40% consistency cap. The $1,000 is withdrawable up to the $1,200 cap, and at a 90% share you keep $900.
EOD trailing drawdown, explained with numbers
The maximum loss limit trails your highest end-of-day balance, not intraday highs. It updates once per day, moves up with profits, and never moves down with losses. On a 50K account, your initial floor is $48,000. Close day one at $51,000 and the floor rises to $49,000; close a later day at $52,000 and the floor reaches $50,000, where it locks. The trailing never exceeds your initial balance, so from that point your worst case is breakeven on the account. If equity touches the floor at any time, the account is breached, which is why intraday risk still matters even though the floor only moves at EOD. Full mechanics are in the Maximum Loss Limit help article.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the FundedNext Futures Rapid Challenge?
FundedNext Futures Rapid is a single-phase futures evaluation offered in two paths, Rapid Pro and Rapid Daily, selected at checkout and locked. It comes in 25K, 50K, and 100K sizes with a one-time fee. Reach the profit target without breaching the EOD trailing maximum loss limit, and you move to a FundedNext Account with a 90% Reward Share.
What is the difference between Futures Rapid Pro and Rapid Daily?
Both pass in one day with a 90% Reward Share. Rapid Pro pays rewards every 3 days with no daily loss limit but a 40% consistency rule in the FundedNext Account; Rapid Daily pays daily with no consistency rule, but adds a daily loss limit and a buffer rule.
Can I switch from Rapid Daily to Rapid Pro after purchasing?
No. The path is locked at purchase and cannot be changed.
Can I really pass in one day?
Yes. With no consistency rule in the challenge, reaching the target in a single session is allowed.
How does the daily loss limit work on Futures Rapid Daily?
It caps how much you can lose in a single trading day: $500 (25K), $1,000 (50K), or $1,250 (100K), and applies from the challenge through the FundedNext Account. Hitting it is not a hard breach: trading is paused for the rest of the session and resumes the next trading day, with the account intact. Only the maximum loss limit ends the account.
Is there a daily loss limit on Futures Rapid Pro?
Not by default. Rapid Pro gives you full intraday flexibility, with only the maximum EOD trailing drawdown controlling risk. If you want a daily guardrail, the optional Daily Loss Limit Add-On applies a limit of $500 (25K), $1,000 (50K), or $1,250 (100K) and reduces the challenge fee. The choice is locked at checkout and cannot be added or removed later. If the limit is hit, it is not a hard breach: trading pauses for the rest of the session and resumes the next trading day, with the account intact.
Is there a consistency rule during the Rapid challenge?
No, on either path. The 40% consistency rule applies only in the Rapid Pro FundedNext Account; Rapid Daily has none at any stage.
What happens if I pass but breach the drawdown before requesting a reward?
The maximum loss limit applies through the entire account journey, so a breach after passing ends the account the same way it would during the challenge. Since a breach means your balance has already fallen back to the trailing floor, the practical takeaway is simple: once you’re reward-eligible, requesting it promptly locks in what you’ve earned.
What platform is used for FundedNext Futures Rapid Challenge?
Futures Rapid runs on Tradovate, NinjaTrader, or TradingView. Platform access and market data are included, with no extra fees.
