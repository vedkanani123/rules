Firm Rules

GENERAL RULES

Minimum Trading Days

Evaluation: 5 days in each phase | To count as a trading day, the trade must remain open for at least 1 minute | No minimum trading days with add-on
Funded: None
News Trading

Evaluation Accounts: No Restriction
Funded Accounts: No trades may be executed on affected instruments within 5 minutes before or after major news events (including pending orders, SL, and TP). Profits made during this 10 min window will be deducted (This is a soft breach), but losses are not compensated. Positions opened more than 5 minutes before the event may be held and modified, though an SL or TP filled within the window still counts as a soft breach. Trades open for at least 48 hours are exempt from profit deduction. Non-targeted instruments can be traded normally.
See details: https://help.brightfunded.com/en/articles/9241694-can-i-trade-news

Copy Trading

Copy trading is allowed only between accounts you personally own (BrightFunded, other prop firms, retail broker). copying trades between different owners’ accounts is strictly prohibited.

BrightFunded’s partnership with Prop Firm One enables you to extend your copy trading capabilities even further. PF1 allows traders to seamlessly copy trades across different proprietary firm accounts within a single platform, offering increased flexibility and a broader range of opportunities. This means that, in addition to BrightFunded’s internal copy trading feature, PF1 empowers users to manage trades across multiple firms, enhancing scalability and trading efficiency without breaching BrightFunded’s core rules. For more details on this enhanced functionality, visit PropFirmOne.com.

Expert Advisors (EAs)

Allowed but must align with trading conditions and rules
While EAs are allowed, not all third-party EAs are compatible with BrightFunded's platform environment.
BrightFunded does not guarantee compatibility or performance of any external EA, and are not responsible for issues caused by non-compatible EAs
30 Days Inactivity Rule

Must place at least one trade every 30 days or account will be deactivated, and if not reactivated via support within 6 months, it will be permanently closed.

Choosing the right challenge is a critical step toward securing a funded trading account. The structure — from account size and profit target to drawdown limits and payout frequency — will directly influence how you trade and manage risk during the evaluation. A challenge that aligns with your strategy can improve your chances of passing and progressing to the funded stage.
