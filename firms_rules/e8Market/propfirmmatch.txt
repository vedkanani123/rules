Consistency Rules

E8 One:

A consistency rule of 40% applies, that means that one trading day can not equal or be greater than 40% of the total profits made. (Funded)
E8 Signature:

A consistency rule of 35% applies, that means that one trading day can not equal or be greater than 35% of the total profits made. (Funded)
Firm Rules

Max Position Size

50 lots per order for FX and indices
20 lots per order for XAUUSD
Independent Evaluation

Each trading evaluation must be completed independently.
Server Request Limitation

There is a limit of 100 orders you can enter at a time, 2000 modifications of TP/SL and orders per day.
The maximum position limit per day is also 2000.
Risk Management

Risking your entire daily drawdown on a single trade is not permitted.
Engaging in prohibited trading practices will result in termination from our program and a refund of the fee paid from the account where this rule was broken.
Hedging

Hedging across multiple accounts, even those belonging to the same trader, is strictly prohibited.
Trade Duration

You cannot hold more than 50% of your trades for under one minute.
Risk Exposure Limitation

E8 reserves the right to de-risk trading strategies by limiting risk exposure to no more than 1% per trade idea or time horizon if prohibited practices are detected.
E8 Trader Stage Account Exceeds Profit Threshold

If your account's profits surpass the Dynamic Drawdown value, you can request a payout of all profits earned. However, you should leave a drawdown buffer in the account to continue trading. If you withdraw all your profits, it will result in the closure of the account.
E8 Pro

2% Daily Profit cap: In each day, the maximum profit you can make is 2%. If you make more, only 2% will be counted towards your profit target.
Static Drawdown: The drawdown does not trail upward and remains fixed at your starting balance until your first payout is requested. Once the first payout is processed, the drawdown is locked at the initial balance level. For every payout request, 50% of the profits are paid out to you, while the remaining 50% stays in the account as a buffer to help maintain the drawdown threshold.
Copy Trading

Copy trading is not allowed between E8 evaluation accounts, as each evaluation must be traded independently; however, you may copy trades between your own personal account, E8 Trader accounts, or between a funded and evaluation account, provided the trades originate from your own accounts.
EAs (Expert Advisors)

Allowed: You can also use any EA (Expert Advisor) as long as we don't see multiple users executing the same trades/strategy. We limit one strategy per user, so if we see multiple users utilize the same EA, it may lead to the termination of your account. So even though the use of third-party EA is allowed, we recommend that each user use their own programmed EA. 
News Trading

Allowed on E8 One evaluation stage, E8 Pro, E8 Signature.
Not allowed in E8 One Funded stage 5 minutes before or after high-impact news.
E8 Signature:

Overnight and weekend trading is not allowed.
Inactivity

After 60 days of inactivity, the account is disabled and there is no entitlement to a refund.

Payout Policy

E8 Signature

A consistency rule of 35% applies, with at least 5 profitable days required, each achieving at least 0.3% profit, along with a buffer equal to the end-of-day trailing drawdown.
Minimum payout amounts: $100
Payout caps and buffer: https://intercom.help/e8/en/articles/11940573-payout-caps-and-buffers-for-e8-signature-explained
E8 One

A consistency rule of 40% and your net profit (payout share) must be greater than 50% of your daily drawdown.
Minimum payout amount: $100
If your profits exceed the Trailing Drawdown, you can request a payout. However, you must leave enough funds as a drawdown buffer, removing all profits will trigger account closure.
E8 Pro

Payouts are available daily, with a minimum profit requirement of 1% and when you request a payout, 50% is your payout, and 50% is kept in the account as a buffer.

Additional Information

A full overview of all the challenges offered by E8 Markets. You'll find up-to-date details for each challenge, including account size, number of steps, profit target, daily and maximum loss limits, profit split, payout frequency, loyalty points, and price. Whether you are new to the firm or comparing different challenge structures, this resource helps you quickly identify the options that fit your trading style, risk tolerance, and goals.

Choosing the right challenge is a critical step toward securing a funded trading account. The structure — from account size and profit target to drawdown limits and payout frequency — will directly influence how you trade and manage risk during the evaluation. A challenge that aligns with your strategy can improve your chances of passing and progressing to the funded stage.

All challenges listed on Prop Firm Match are based on verified information from the firms themselves and are updated regularly to ensure accuracy. Our goal is to give traders a clear, side-by-side view of the available options, making it easier to compare not only across challenges from the same firm but also with those from other top prop firms.
