Firm Rules

News Trading

Allowed in all Challenge Phases
Funded Accounts and Instant Funding: Executing orders (including opened or closed orders, pending orders, SL/TP triggered) 2 minutes before and 2 minutes after high impact news on affected instruments is prohibited. Any profits made within the window will be voided.
Copy Trading

Inactivity Rule

Evaluation: 90 days
Funded: 365 days
Expert Advisor (EA)

Traders may use an Expert Advisor (EA) as long as they own it and aligns with their strategy. The use of commercial EAs is strictly prohibited.

Allowed

Not Allowed

Overleveraging

Using 85% or more of the margin on a single asset is prohibited.
Group Trading / Account Management / Account Sharing

Coordinated trading across two or more accounts of multiple users is strongly prohibited.
Chasing Price Rule (90 Seconds)

Reversing positions on the same asset within 90 seconds of closing a prior trade is prohibited. This includes rapidly switching between buy and sell to recover losses without a structured strategy.
Anti-Gambling Policy

Forbidden Gambling practices are: Aggressive Averaging, Martingale and One-Sided Betting, Chasing Price (90 Seconds Rule), Lack of Structured Risk Management, Tick Scalping, Grid Trading, Hyperactivity / Execution Flooding.

Top Firm Aspect
What stands out most about a solid prop firm comes down to three key things: Execution and Spreads: Clean order execution during high-volatility events without unexpected slippage or artificial spread widening makes trading strategy execution reliable. Payout Reliability and Speed: A firm's true stability shows when it processes withdrawal requests smoothly and strictly honors its published terms without delays or shifting rules. Clear, Fair Rules: Straightforward drawdown limits (especially static over trailing drawdowns) and transparent evaluation rules give traders the environment they need to scale capital safely. If you have a specific firm in mind, sharing what features they offer—like their profit split, drawdown type, or news trading rules—can help highlight what aligns best with your trading style.

Least Liked Aspect
What often frustrates traders about prop firms comes down to strict rules, friction, and hidden fine print: Strict & Complex Drawdown Rules: Trailing drawdowns (especially those calculated based on equity rather than balance) can prematurely blow an account during open trade drawdown, leaving little room for normal market fluctuations. Inflexible Trading Restrictions: Restrictive rules—such as hard caps on lot sizes, mandatory stop-loss requirements, bans on trading through high-impact news events, or strict weekend holding rules—can feel unnecessarily punitive for discretionary traders. Payout Delays & Bureaucracy: Delayed withdrawal processing times, strict minimum trading day requirements before your first payout, or high payout thresholds create unnecessary friction when trying to collect profits. Platform Latency & Slippage: Spreading spreads, execution delays, or slippage around market open/close times can turn an edge negative.
