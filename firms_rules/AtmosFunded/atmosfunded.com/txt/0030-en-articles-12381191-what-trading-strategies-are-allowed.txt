But to protect traders and ensure fair conditions, there are rules regarding certain events, strategies, and trades.⚡ OverviewYou can use EAs, swing/ intraday strategies. You cannot trade high‐impact news ±2 minutes, use disallowed strategies, or violate open risk limits. 🧰 Permitted vs Prohibited StrategiesPermittedProhibited / RestrictedManual trading – scalping, swing, intradayNews trading within ±2 minutes of high‐impact eventsExpert Advisors (EAs)Unapproved proprietary EAs (e.g. arbitrage or HFT)Holding trades overnight or over weekendsHedging across accounts (if policy prohibits)⚙️ News Trading PolicyHolding trades through news events is allowed generally, but trades opened or closed within 2 minutes before or after high‐impact economic events is highly prohibited.
