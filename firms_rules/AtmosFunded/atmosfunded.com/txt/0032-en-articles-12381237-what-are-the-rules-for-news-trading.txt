Updated over 2 weeks agoTable of contentsAtmos restricts trading during high-impact economic news events to ensure fair execution and prevent excessive risk.⚡ OverviewYou cannot open or close trades within ±2 minutes of high-impact news releases. Holding trades through news is allowed unless otherwise specified. 📊 News Trading Policy🚫 No opening new positions within 2 minutes before or after major news events.🚫 No closing positions specifically to profit from slippage/gap within that window.✅ Holding an existing trade through the news is permitted (at your own risk).💡 ExampleNFP (Non-Farm Payrolls) release at 14:30 GMTForbidden trading window: 14:28 → 14:32 GMT✅ If you opened a trade at 12:00 and hold through the news → allowed.❌ If a position triggers an opening, closing, pending order, TP/SL at 14:29 → breach.Related ArticlesWhat trading strategies are allowed?Can I Hold Trades Overnight and Over Weekends?Are Hedging Strategies Allowed?Can I Use Expert Advisors (EA)What Are Prohibited Strategies?Did this answer your question?😞😐😃Table of contentsAtmosFunded  Help CenterBroker-backed prop firm.
