How to become an Affiliate?

Updated over 2 weeks ago
Table of contents

Blue Guardian Affiliate Program

The Blue Guardian Affiliate Program allows you to earn commissions by referring traders to Blue Guardian. Every time a trader signs up and purchases an evaluation through your unique affiliate link or coupon code, you earn a commission.

How do I become an affiliate?

You can sign up through our affiliate portal:

https://blueguardian.com/affiliates

Once your application is approved, you will receive access to your affiliate dashboard where you can manage your links, track referrals, and request payouts.

How much can I earn?

Commission rates are based on affiliate level:

  • Level 1: 10%

  • Level 2: 12.5%

  • Level 3: 15%

Commissions are calculated based on every successful sale made through your affiliate link or coupon code.

How and when do payouts work?

  • Minimum payout threshold: $200

  • Payout schedule: Anytime after reaching the $200 minimum payout

  • Payout requests can be submitted directly through your affiliate dashboard.

Can I track my referrals and earnings?

Yes. Your affiliate dashboard provides real-time tracking for:

  • Referrals

  • Sales conversions

  • Earnings

  • Campaign performance

You can also create multiple tracking links to monitor different campaigns separately.

Do I need to be a trader to join?

No. Anyone can apply for the affiliate program. You do not need to be an active trader.

Is there a cost to join?

No. The Blue Guardian Affiliate Program is completely free to join.

Where can I promote my affiliate link?

You may promote your affiliate links on:

  • Facebook

  • Instagram

  • X (Twitter)

  • YouTube

  • TikTok

  • Other social media platforms

Can I run paid ads?

Paid advertising is allowed under our terms, however:

❌ Google Ads / PPC advertising is strictly prohibited and may result in affiliate account closure.

How long does referral confirmation take?

Referrals are usually confirmed within 24–48 hours after the purchase is successfully completed.

Can I refer myself?

No. Self-referrals are not allowed. Affiliate commissions are only paid for genuine third-party referrals.

How does the affiliate cookie policy work?

Blue Guardian operates on a 30-day cookie policy.

This means if a trader clicks on your affiliate link, a cookie will be stored in their browser for 30 days. If they complete a purchase within that period, the commission will be credited to your affiliate account.

Please note: if the trader’s browser already has an active cookie from another affiliate’s referral link, the commission will automatically be assigned to the affiliate linked to that existing cookie.

Does the Rewards Program apply to custom affiliate deals?

No. The Rewards Program does not apply to affiliates with custom deals or individually negotiated agreements.

Affiliates on custom terms will continue to receive the benefits outlined in their specific agreement and are not eligible for additional Rewards Program benefits.
