Traders can qualify for a funded account by reaching the required profit target while complying with all trading rules and risk management requirements.Once the challenge has been successfully completed, traders simply pay the remaining balance to receive their funded account. Below is a complete overview of the rules, trading conditions, and funded account requirements for the Buy Now, Pay Later model.Profit TargetTraders must achieve a 4% profit target while complying with all trading rules and risk management requirements. Once the profit target has been reached, the account will undergo a final review. After successful verification and payment of the remaining balance, the trader will receive their Funded Account and can begin trading as a funded Blue Guardian trader.Drawdown LimitsMaximum Daily LossThe maximum daily loss is limited to 4% of the initial account balance.This value resets daily at 5pm EST and is calculated using the higher value between the account balance or equity at the time of the reset. We calculate daily drawdown as follows: Upon the 5pm Est day change, we will take the higher figure of either the account balance or account equity and subtract a fixed 4% of the initial balance from the higher value as the daily loss limit for the new day. It is the maximum amount of loss allowed within a single trading day, which the account will be breached when daily loss exceeds this level.​​Example 1:On a 100K Account, if at 5pm EST you have an open trade with a floating profit of $2,000, your account equity will be 102K. With a 4% daily drawdown, the equity cannot drop below $98,000 ($102,000 - $4,000 = $98,000) on the next trading day.​​Example 2:If on a 100K Account at 5pm EST you have an open trade with a floating loss of -$2,000, your account equity will be $98,000. Because your $100K balance is higher than your $98,000 equity, your daily drawdown limit of $4,000 will be calculated on a $100K balance. This means for the next trading day, the equity cannot drop below $96,000 ($100,000 - $4000 = $96,000).‍​Example 3:If there are no open trades at 5pm EST, the daily drawdown will be 4% of the initial balance is.Maximum Overall Drawdown (Trailing)BPNL maximum drawdown will remain at 8% trailing of your Highest Watermark Closing Trade made. If your account reaches 8% of your Initial balance your trailing Drawdown will lock in at your starting balance.​Once your Drawdown is locked in at your starting balance we add a fixed 1% buffer on the account. This way you cannot withdraw all the profit and breach your account.​Example 1: You have a 100k account. Your Daily drawdown is a fixed 4% and your Max Trailing is 8%. Your Daily drawdown level is $96,000 and your Max trailing is $92,000. If you make $12,000 profit, once your account has made over 8% of the Initial balance your Trailing now locks in at $100,000. It no longer trails. Drawdown includes profits and losses from closed positions, swap fees and commissions‍Example 2: You start out with a 100k account. Your Daily drawdown is 4% and your Max Trailing is 8%. Your Daily drawdown level is $96,000 and your Max Trailing is $92,000. Lets say you make $5,000 profit. This puts your account at $105,000. The next day your Daily drawdown will be $105,000- $4000 = $101,000. Your Max trailing drawdown is $105,000-$8,000 = $97,000. Your new Max trailing drawdown is $97,000 because it trails from your highest recorded balance which was $105,000.​Daily drawdown will reset each Day at 5PM EST. Max Trailing will always be from Highest recorded balance unless you achieve 6% from your Initial balance.Minimum Trading DaysThere is no minimum trading day requirement to pass the evaluation, the minimum trading day requirement only applies to Funded Accounts before a payout can be requested. To become eligible for a payout, traders must complete at least 5 trading days. A trading day is counted on any day where the account generates a minimum profit of 0.5% of the initial account balance. The 5 trading days do not need to be consecutive.News Trading RuleWe allow news trading on challenge.​However, we do not allow news trading on funded accounts. For trades executed on your Funded Account, please refrain from Opening/Closing trades 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after red folder (high-impact) news events. Any profits earned during this time or significantly influenced by news events will be subject to removal, without any account violation this also extends to trades that are maliciously attempting to bypass rule.​Additionally, please refrain from Opening/Closing trades 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after FOMC speeches, statements, and news events on Funded accounts. You may use EAs (Expert Advisors) that you setup to suit your own strategy or trading style.​While VPNs and VPS are allowed, using either service to bypass rules related to copy trading, group trading, or signal trading is strictly prohibited. If you are found misusing these services in violation of your contract or our Terms of Use, your account will be failed and terminated.Tick ScalpingThe minimum holding time is 2 minutes. If a trade is closed in under 2 minutes, it may be flagged for tick scalping, which is against our trading rules.Can I hold overnight & trade over weekends?We have no restrictions on holding trades over night or the weekend on all account types.What is the Guardian Shield?Our analysis shows that exceeding the Maximum Daily Drawdown is the main cause of account breaches, accounting for over 73% of all breaches. Our most profitable traders are those who cut losses quickly and let winning trades run. The Guardian Shield encourages proper risk management for consistent profitability.​​This only applies on BPNL Funded Accounts:​If your PnL on open trades reaches a 1% loss, Guardian Shield will, in most cases, automatically close all open trades for all symbols. This is a soft breach, and you may immediately begin trading afterward.​‍Example:‍You have a balance of $100,000 funded account and 3 open trades. Your open PnL reaches a total loss of $1,000 (1%). The Guardian Shield will automatically close all trades. You can continue trading as you like afterwards.​Guardian Shield is an important risk management tool that helps prevent traders from blowing their funded accounts, and ultimately helps create more consistent profits with increased risk management.​​Guardian Shield Breach Consequences:• 1st Breach: Profit split reduced to 50%.• 2nd Breach: The account is permanently breached.​​Important Notice: The Guardian Shield does not reset once triggered and the profit split cannot be reinstated. These changes are permanent and cannot be reversed.Withdrawal Buffer RulesBPNL includes a 1% withdrawal buffer, which is activated once the account reaches 8% profit and the trailing drawdown locks at breakeven. Once the account reaches 8% profit, a 1% buffer of the initial balance must remain in the account when requesting a payout.Key points:🔹 Minimum withdrawal amount: $100🔹 Traders are not required to reach 8% profit before requesting a payout. Payouts can be requested at any time, provided all withdrawal rules are met. This buffer is in place to prevent traders from withdrawing the full available drawdown and unintentionally breaching their account.How does BPNL Drawdown affect payouts?On BPNL accounts, the Maximum Drawdown is a 8% trailing drawdown based on your highest closed balance (High Watermark).Once your account reaches 8% profit from the initial balance, the trailing drawdown will lock at your starting balance. At this stage, a 1% fixed buffer applies, meaning you cannot withdraw all profits and leave the account at the locked drawdown level.Examples:Example 1You start with a $100,000 account.Daily Drawdown: $96,000Max Trailing Drawdown: $92,000You make $10,000 profit and request a $8,000 payout.Since you achieved 8%, your max drawdown locks at $100,000. After withdrawal, your balance is $102,000, leaving you with only 2% buffer before breaching.Example 2You make $20,000 profit and request an $13,000 payout.Your drawdown locks at $100,000, and after payout your balance is $107,000, leaving you with a $7,000 buffer.Example 3You make exactly $8,000 profit.Because of the 1% fixed buffer rule, the maximum you can withdraw is $7,000. This leaves your balance at $101,000, while your drawdown remains locked at $100,000.
