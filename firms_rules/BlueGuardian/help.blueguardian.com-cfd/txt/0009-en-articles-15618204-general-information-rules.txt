Our evaluation models include: 1 Step Pro, 1 Step Standard, 1 Step Crypto, 2 Step Standard, 2 Step Pro and 3 Step. For traders who want to skip the evaluation phase, we also offer Instant Accounts and Instant Starter Accounts, which provide immediate access to funded trading. To trade a funded account through the evaluation route, traders must successfully complete their selected evaluation model. Once passed, a funded account will be issued. We currently offer 9 account sizes: $5k, $10k, $25k, $50k, $100k, $150k, $200k, $300k, and $400k. The selected account size represents the available balance you will trade with throughout the process.Maximum Allocation Funding​You may purchase and trade as many challenge accounts as you like, with no limit on the number of evaluations you can hold.However, the maximum active funded allocation allowed at any time is $400,000 across all funded account types.This means you can hold multiple funded accounts, provided the combined balance does not exceed the $400,000 allocation limit. Example:If you already have a $200,000 Instant Funding account, you may only hold an additional $200,000 in instant account or funded accounts across any other plans.There is no limit on how many challenge accounts you can purchase, but only up to $400,000 can be actively funded at once. Through our Scaling Plan, traders can grow their allocation up to $2,000,000 over time. Scaling PlanYes, we offer a Scaling Plan that allows traders to grow their accounts up to a maximum of $4,000,000. To qualify for scaling, you must achieve 12% profit within a 3-month period while remaining in compliance with all trading rules. Once eligible, your account size will be increased by 30% of your initial balance, allowing you to steadily grow your capital over time.Are there any age and Countries restriction?You must be 18 years old or older to purchase. Restricted countries from using are services include:AfghanistanAlbaniaAlgeriaCubaIranJordanLibyaMyanmarNorth KoreaPhilippinesSenegalSyriaVietnam​Payment MethodsWe currently accept Debit/Credit Cards and Cryptocurrency for all evaluation purchases. When does my Evaluation start?Your Evaluation begins from the moment you place your first trade on the account.Please note that while the trading period starts with your first trade, our inactivity rule begins from the moment the account is created. Inactivity RuleTo keep your account active, you must place at least one trade every 30 days.If no trade is placed within this period, the account will be considered inactive and will result in a breach.We recommend monitoring your account activity to ensure compliance. Am I trading with real money?No. All accounts provided by Blue Guardian, including funded accounts, are demo accounts operating in a simulated trading environment with virtual funds. Our funded accounts use real market quotes sourced from liquidity providers to replicate live market conditions as closely as possible. This allows traders to experience realistic execution and market behavior while trading in a simulated environment. Funded accounts should not be considered live trading accounts with real capital. What is the legal relationship with Blue Guardian and a Funded Trader?The relationship between a Funded Trader and Blue Guardian is governed by the Blue Guardian Trader Agreement, which is issued once you successfully complete the Evaluation process. This agreement outlines the terms, conditions, and responsibilities between both parties and must be signed before a funded account is issued. The Blue Guardian Trader Agreement is a legally binding contract and serves as the official framework for your relationship with Blue Guardian as a Funded Trader. I have passed the Evaluation Program, what is next?Once you successfully pass your Evaluation, your funded account credentials will be automatically sent to your email within 24 business hours along with your Trader Agreement and Funded Trader Certificate. KYC VerificationInstant Starter Accounts: No KYC requiredAll other Instant & Funded Accounts: KYC is only required before submitting your first payout requestPlease see the documents required for identity verification in your country using the link https://sumsub.com/supported-documents/​If your document is allowed, you can complete your KYC verification through the Verification tab on your dashboard. How does the refund fee work at Blue Guardian?The evaluation fee for completed accounts is fully refundable after your fourth payout (excluding Instant Starter accounts).If your account is hard-breached before reaching the fourth payout, the fee will not be refunded.
