After successful verification, the trader will move forward as a Guardian/Funded Trader.Drawdown LimitsMaximum Daily LossThe maximum daily loss is limited to 3% of the initial account balance.This value resets daily at 5pm EST and is calculated using the higher value between the account balance or equity at the time of the reset. We calculate daily drawdown as follows: Upon the 5pm Est day change, we will take the higher figure of either the account balance or account equity and subtract a fixed 3% of the initial balance from the higher value as the daily loss limit for the new day. It is the maximum amount of loss allowed within a single trading day, which the account will be breached when daily loss exceeds this level.​​Example 1:On a 100K Account, if at 5pm EST you have an open trade with a floating profit of $2,000, your account equity will be 102K. With a 3% daily drawdown, the equity cannot drop below $99,000 ($102,000 - $3,000 = $99,000) on the next trading day.​​Example 2:If on a 100K Account at 5pm EST you have an open trade with a floating loss of -$2,000, your account equity will be $98,000. Because your $100K balance is higher than your $98,000 equity, your daily drawdown limit of $3,000 will be calculated on a $100K balance. This means for the next trading day, the equity cannot drop below $97,000 ($100,000 - $3000 = $97,000).‍​Example 3:If there are no open trades at 5pm EST, the daily drawdown will be 3% of the initial balance is.Maximum Overall Drawdown (Static)The maximum total loss allowed in 2 Step Nano account is 10%.‍​Example using a $100,000 account:Max total loss is 10% of your initial balance = $10,000. Your account equity including unrealized/floating positions must not hit/exceed below $90,000.Minimum Trading DaysThere is no minimum trading day requirement for either the Evaluation (Challenge) or Funded Account.Traders can pass the evaluation or become eligible for a payout as soon as all other applicable requirements and trading rules are met.Consistency Rule A 50% consistency rule applies to Funded Accounts only. This means that your highest profit from a single trading day cannot be equal to or greater than 50% of your total profits during that payout period. If your highest profit day is equal to or greater than 50%, your account will not be breached or terminated. You will simply need to continue trading and generate additional profits until your highest profit day falls below 50% of your total profits. Example: If you make $1,000 in one trading day, your total profit must exceed $2,000 before you meet the 50% consistency requirement and become eligible to request a payout.News Trading RuleWe allow news trading on challenge.​However, we do not allow news trading on funded accounts. For trades executed on your Funded Account, please refrain from Opening/Closing trades 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after red folder (high-impact) news events. Any profits earned during this time or significantly influenced by news events will be subject to removal, without any account violation this also extends to trades that are maliciously attempting to bypass rule.​Additionally, please refrain from Opening/Closing trades 5 minutes before and 5 minutes after FOMC speeches, statements, and news events on Funded accounts. Any trades involving FOMC events will be subject to profit removal, as well. If you are found misusing these services in violation of your contract or our Terms of Use, your account will be failed and terminated.Tick ScalpingThe minimum holding time is 2 minutes.
