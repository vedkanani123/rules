Whether you’re new to futures trading or an experienced trader, Blue Guardian Futures is built to provide the structure, capital, and support needed to help you succeed. At Blue Guardian Futures, we are more than just a prop firm. We are committed to building long-term relationships with traders by offering transparent rules, flexible trading models, fast support and scalable funded opportunities.Our mission is simple: To help disciplined traders gain access to larger capital while developing sustainable and professional trading habits.Why Blue Guardian Futures?Blue Guardian Futures is built around flexibility, speed and trader-first systems.We understand that every trader has a different style, which is why our programs are structured to accommodate both conservative and aggressive trading approaches. One-Phase Evaluation ProcessOur evaluation model is designed to be simple and straightforward.Rather than forcing traders through multiple phases, Blue Guardian Futures uses a one-phase evaluation system, allowing traders to qualify faster by reaching their required profit targets while remaining compliant with all account rules.This creates a faster pathway to funded trading.Blue Guardian Futures offers multiple account models designed for different trading styles and goals.These include:Standard Accounts — Structured accounts with fixed contract limits and consistent payout opportunities.Reserve Accounts — Flexible funded accounts with no daily loss limits and higher payout flexibility.Express Accounts — Designed for traders who want faster payout frequency and daily cash flow opportunities.Direct Accounts (Instant Funding) — Immediate access to a funded account with no evaluation required.Whether you prefer to earn your way through evaluation or start instantly funded, Blue Guardian Futures provides options that suit your strategy.Funded traders receive:Access to larger capitalFlexible payout structures depending on the account modelCompetitive profit split opportunitiesScaling opportunitiesEligibility for Live Account progressionBlue Guardian Futures rewards consistency, discipline and long-term performance.One-Time Fee StructureAll Blue Guardian Futures accounts operate on a one-time purchase model.This applies to:Evaluation AccountsReserve AccountsExpress AccountsDirect (Instant) AccountsThis means:No monthly subscription feesNo recurring renewal feesNo hidden activation feesFor Direct (Instant) Accounts, traders receive immediate access to a funded account after purchase. For evaluation-based accounts, once you successfully pass, there are no additional activation fees required before receiving your funded account. You pay once and trade.Fast Payout ProcessingAt Blue Guardian Futures, we understand the importance of fast access to profits.That is why all payout requests are processed within 24 business hours, provided all payout conditions have been met.Available payout methods include:CryptoRise WorksPayout requests are submitted directly through your dashboard.Please note that payout schedules vary depending on your account model, giving traders flexibility based on their chosen plan.
