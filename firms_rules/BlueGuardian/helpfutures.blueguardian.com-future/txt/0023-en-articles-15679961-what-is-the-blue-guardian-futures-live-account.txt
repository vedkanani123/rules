Live Accounts represent the final progression within the Blue Guardian Futures ecosystem and are earned through performance—not purchased. Once promoted, traders gain access to real capital, daily payouts, no consistency requirements, and additional incentives designed to reward professional trading.How do I become eligible for a Live Account?Live Accounts are not automatically awarded and cannot be purchased.Instead, traders become eligible for Live Review after demonstrating consistent performance on funded accounts.A trader may enter the Live Review Pool when one or more of the following conditions are met:Successfully reaching Payout 5 on a funded account.Receiving a significant amount of lifetime payouts.Demonstrating exceptional performance on funded accounts.Having previously traded on a Blue Guardian Live Account.ImportantReaching Payout 5 does not guarantee promotion to a Live Account.It simply places the trader into the Live Review Pool, where the Blue Guardian Futures Risk Team evaluates whether the trader is ready to transition to live capital.All Live promotions are made solely at the discretion of the Blue Guardian Futures Risk Team.How does the Live Review process work?Every trader entering the Live Review Pool undergoes an internal assessment by the Blue Guardian Futures Risk Team.During the review, the team considers factors including:Overall trading consistencyRisk managementTrading disciplineLifetime profitabilityAccount behaviourPrevious Live account history (where applicable)The objective is to identify traders capable of managing real capital responsibly over the long term.What happens when I am promoted to Live?Once approved, your eligible funded accounts will automatically transition into Live Accounts.For each eligible funded account, you will receive one corresponding Live Account.Each Live Account begins with:$0 Starting BalanceReal CME executionDaily payoutsEnd-of-Day drawdownNo daily loss limitNo additional purchase or activation fee is required.Do all funded accounts move to Live?Yes.When a trader is approved for Live, all eligible funded accounts will transition together.To be considered eligible, each funded account must have received at least one successful payout.ExampleIf you currently have:2 Standard Funded Accounts1 Reserve Funded Account2 Express Funded Accountsand each account has received at least one payout, all five accounts will move to Live together.What happens to my remaining simulated accounts?Once you transition to Live:All active Evaluation Accounts will be closed.Any funded account that has not yet received a payout will not move to Live.If a funded account is ineligible because it has received zero payouts, the evaluation fee paid for that account will be refunded.Refunds for Evaluation Accounts and Funded Accounts that have not yet received a payout are subject to the account’s remaining maximum drawdown:If the account has more than 50% of its maximum drawdown remaining, your evaluation fee will be refunded.If the account has 50% or less of its maximum drawdown remaining, the account will be closed without a refund. If you intend to continue using evaluation accounts, we recommend not holding unused evaluations when approaching Live eligibility.Live Account SpecificationsFunded Account SizeStarting Live DrawdownMaximum Position Size$25,000$1,0002 Minis / 20 Micros$50,000$2,0004 Minis / 40 Micros$100,000$3,0006 Minis / 60 Micros$150,000$4,50010 Minis / 100 MicrosPlease note: Certain highly volatile contracts may have reduced position limits to ensure traders remain within the available drawdown.Live Account RulesOnce promoted, Live Accounts operate under the following conditions.Profit SplitTrader receives 90%Blue Guardian receives 10%Payout FrequencyDaily payouts.No payout caps.DrawdownLive Accounts use an End-of-Day (EOD) Drawdown model.If a payout is requested before your account balance exceeds the live drawdown amount, your Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) will lock to $100. Daily Loss LimitThere is no daily loss limit on Live Accounts. Consistency RuleThere are no consistency requirements on Live Accounts.Regardless of whether your account originated from the Standard, Reserve, Express, or Direct model, all consistency requirements are removed once you transition to Live. News TradingNews trading is not permitted on Live Accounts due to the risks associated with real CME order execution during high-impact news events. HedgingHedging is strictly prohibited on Live Accounts.Violations may result in permanent account closure and a permanent ban from Blue Guardian Futures. Household RuleIf one member of a household is actively trading a Live Account, other household members may not trade simulated Blue Guardian Futures accounts.Live Scaling PlanLive Accounts use a progressive scaling system that adjusts your maximum contract limits based on the profits generated on your Live Account. Scaling is reviewed at the end of each trading day (EOD). As your profits increase, additional contract limits become available. If your profits decrease, your maximum position size may also be reduced accordingly.Please note that contract limits also vary depending on the exchange and product traded. Blue Guardian reserves the right to update Live funding eligibility without prior notice.Is reaching Payout 5 a guarantee that I will receive a Live Account?Reaching Payout 5 places you into the Live Review Pool, but it does not guarantee promotion to a Live Account. Every trader is individually assessed by the Blue Guardian Futures Risk Team.Can I purchase a Live Account?Live Accounts cannot be purchased and are only earned through consistent trading performance.Can I buy back a breached Live Account?Live Accounts cannot be reset, repurchased, or reactivated.If you breach a Live Account, you must earn another opportunity through the funded program.What happens if I breach my Live Account?If a Live Account is breached:A standard 2-week cooldown applies before purchasing a new evaluation.Traders who repeatedly breach Live Accounts through reckless or excessive risk-taking may be subject to longer cooldown periods at the discretion of the Blue Guardian Futures Risk Team.Need additional assistance?If you have any questions regarding the Blue Guardian Futures Live Account Program, please contact our support team through Live Chat or by emailing [email protected].
