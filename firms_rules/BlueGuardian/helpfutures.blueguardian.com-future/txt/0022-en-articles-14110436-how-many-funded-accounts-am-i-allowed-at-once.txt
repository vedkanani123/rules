July 6, 2026You may purchase as many Evaluation accounts as you wish. However, your total funded accounts cannot exceed 5 accounts to be traded simultaneously.  If purchasing a Direct account or qualifying for a new Funded account would cause you to exceed this limit, the additional account will remain inactive until space becomes available within your active funded allocation.  Once an active funded account is closed, breached or otherwise no longer counts toward your allocation, the next eligible account can be activated.
