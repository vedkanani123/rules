Introduction to Blue Guardian Futures

July 10, 2026
Table of contents

Welcome to Blue Guardian Futures.

We’re excited to have you join us on this trading journey. Whether you’re new to futures trading or an experienced trader, Blue Guardian Futures is built to provide the structure, capital, and support needed to help you succeed.

At Blue Guardian Futures, we are more than just a prop firm. We are committed to building long-term relationships with traders by offering transparent rules, flexible trading models, fast support and scalable funded opportunities.

Our mission is simple:

To help disciplined traders gain access to larger capital while developing sustainable and professional trading habits.

Why Blue Guardian Futures?

Blue Guardian Futures is built around flexibility, speed and trader-first systems.

We understand that every trader has a different style, which is why our programs are structured to accommodate both conservative and aggressive trading approaches.

One-Phase Evaluation Process

Our evaluation model is designed to be simple and straightforward.

Rather than forcing traders through multiple phases, Blue Guardian Futures uses a one-phase evaluation system, allowing traders to qualify faster by reaching their required profit targets while remaining compliant with all account rules.

This creates a faster pathway to funded trading.

Blue Guardian Futures offers multiple account models designed for different trading styles and goals.

These include:

  • Standard Accounts — Structured accounts with fixed contract limits and consistent payout opportunities.

  • Reserve Accounts — Flexible funded accounts with no daily loss limits and higher payout flexibility.

  • Express Accounts — Designed for traders who want faster payout frequency and daily cash flow opportunities.

  • Direct Accounts (Instant Funding) — Immediate access to a funded account with no evaluation required.

Whether you prefer to earn your way through evaluation or start instantly funded, Blue Guardian Futures provides options that suit your strategy.

Funded traders receive:

  • Access to larger capital

  • Flexible payout structures depending on the account model

  • Competitive profit split opportunities

  • Scaling opportunities

  • Eligibility for Live Account progression

Blue Guardian Futures rewards consistency, discipline and long-term performance.

One-Time Fee Structure

All Blue Guardian Futures accounts operate on a one-time purchase model.

This applies to:

  • Evaluation Accounts

  • Reserve Accounts

  • Express Accounts

  • Direct (Instant) Accounts

This means:

  • No monthly subscription fees

  • No recurring renewal fees

  • No hidden activation fees

For Direct (Instant) Accounts, traders receive immediate access to a funded account after purchase. For evaluation-based accounts, once you successfully pass, there are no additional activation fees required before receiving your funded account. You pay once and trade.

Fast Payout Processing

At Blue Guardian Futures, we understand the importance of fast access to profits.

That is why all payout requests are processed within 24 business hours, provided all payout conditions have been met.

Available payout methods include:

  • Crypto

  • Rise Works

Payout requests are submitted directly through your dashboard.

Please note that payout schedules vary depending on your account model, giving traders flexibility based on their chosen plan. Please ensure all payout details and KYC requirements are completed before submitting to avoid delays.

Can I Use Someone Else’s Card?

Blue Guardian Futures requires all purchases to be made using a payment method that belongs to the same person who owns and trades the account.

Third-party payment methods are not accepted under any circumstances.

Important Conditions

The following must match:

  • Registered account name

  • Cardholder name

  • Billing address

If mismatched payment methods are detected, this may result in:

  • Account termination

  • Payout denial

  • Refund denial

  • Permanent ban

Fraud & Chargebacks

Fraudulent activity involving payment methods will not be tolerated.

This includes:

  • Unauthorized card usage

  • Chargeback abuse

  • Payment manipulation

  • False payment disputes

Blue Guardian Futures maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward payment fraud. Any violations may result in permanent removal from the platform.

KYC / AML Policy

To maintain platform security and regulatory compliance, Blue Guardian Futures follows strict Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) procedures.

These checks protect both traders and the company.

When is KYC Required?

KYC verification is required for:

  • Trader payout requests

  • Affiliate payout requests

KYC verification must be successfully completed before any payout can be processed.KYC is completed once per user and only one verified profile is allowed per individual.

Required Documents

Proof of Identity (PoI)

Accepted documents:

  • Passport

  • National Identity Card

  • Driver’s License

Documents must be valid and unexpired.

Live Selfie / Video Verification

Users must complete a live selfie or video verification that matches their submitted identity document. This confirms ownership of the document.

Proof of Address (PoA)

Accepted documents include:

  • Bank statements

  • Utility bills

  • Government-issued correspondence

  • Lease agreements

  • Credit card statements

  • Employer verification letters

  • House purchase deeds

Requirements:

  • Must be dated within the last 3 months

  • Must clearly display your full address

Alternative PoA documents may be accepted where applicable.

AML Screening

All users undergo AML screening, including checks against:

  • Global sanctions lists

  • OFAC watchlists

  • PEP databases

  • Adverse media reports

This process is mandatory.

Restricted Countries

Blue Guardian Futures cannot offer services to certain restricted countries due to:

  • International sanctions

  • Regulatory restrictions

  • Elevated fraud risks

Users located in restricted jurisdictions may not be eligible to open or maintain accounts.

Ongoing Verification Monitoring

Blue Guardian Futures continuously monitors identity documents through its third-party verification provider.

To remain compliant:

  • Documents must remain valid

  • Expired documents may require resubmission

  • Failed verification may delay payouts

All verification decisions made by our third-party provider are final and cannot be overridden by Blue Guardian Futures.

Account Inactivity

To keep your account active, you must place at least one trade within the required inactivity period.

  • Evaluation Accounts: At least one trade is required every 30 calendar days.

  • Funded Accounts: At least one trade is required every 7 calendar days.

Failure to place a trade within the applicable timeframe will result in the account being considered inactive and breached.
