SECTION 5 — MODEL-BY-MODEL RULES
================================================================================
--- 5A. PRACTICE (Evaluation) ---
- ONE RULE: ALTV/LTMAB trails OPEN EQUITY intra-trade on unrealized highs.
  Touch = auto-liquidate + restricted trading (admins-only popup on Rithmic;
  Tradovate notifications). Resets do NOT revive without paid reset.
- Example 8-contract: $2,500 DD trails every unrealized peak until stopped.
- Expiration-date renewal does NOT restore balance/failed status; reset needed.
- Failing does NOT auto-cancel subscription; cancel to avoid rebill (irreversible).
--- 5B. PERFORMANCE ACCOUNT (PA) ---
- 30% rule: daily profits must not exceed 30% of net profit from start.
- No-flip: <5% of days may be flip/minimal-activity days; 12 trading days/month
  required on $250 one-time PA plan (minimal-trade gaming banned).
- Max DD same ALTV engine; hold max 3 micros past close without permission;
  standard lots need admin approval + margin cover; flat 15 min before close.
- Copy-trading another user / letting others trade = closure. SIM abuse banned.
- Manual discretionary + indicators allowed; entry/exit bots per plan terms.
