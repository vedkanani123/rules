SECTION 8 — PROHIBITIONS & TRADING RESTRICTIONS (official + reviewer-noted)
================================================================================
1. All-or-Nothing / gambling rule: margin utilization consistently above ~20%
   of account = flaggable "reckless" trading; can deny payouts / close
   accounts (top complaint source — size positions conservatively).
2. HFT, arbitrage, latency exploitation: banned (all plans, incl. EAs).
3. Martingale/grid: banned per TheTrustedProp prohibited list (single-account
   classic hedging reported permitted — confirm T&Cs).
4. Hedging across multiple Lark accounts / group hedging: banned.
5. Copy trading: eval = ONE external -> ONE eval only; funded may inter-copy.
   Third-party management / signal-passing services: banned.
6. Stock CFDs through earnings/dividend releases: banned.
7. VPN spoofing / jurisdiction evasion: banned.
8. Weekend hold without add-on: forced close Fri 3:45 PM EST (not a "ban"
   but auto-liquidation).
9. Inactivity: 30 days no trades = inactive/terminated.
10. Smart-Reset discipline: single-trade loss >1% or DD >=5% voids free reset
    (not an account breach by itself).
Consequences ladder: payout denial, account closure, termination.
