--- 5A. EVALUATION EOD-TRAILING ---

  on closed balance, enforced real-time next session incl. unrealized.
- Daily Loss Limit (EOD only): pauses the day, does not end account.
- Rithmic freeze: trailing stops at profit target (Rithmic only); Tradovate trails forever.
- No eval consistency rule. Full contracts allowed. No time pressure beyond 30-day access.
- Breach = fail; no resets since Mar 2026 — must buy new eval.
--- 5B. EVALUATION INTRADAY-TRAILING ---
- Same targets/amounts but floor follows highest EQUITY incl. open unrealized
  in real time (legacy mechanic, aggressive). No DLL. For traders preferring
  aggressive structure; most should choose EOD.
--- 5C. EVALUATION STATIC 100K ---
- $2,000 target, $625 fixed floor (start minus $625, never moves). 2 minis max.
  Predictable, tight; cheapest entry.
--- 5D. PERFORMANCE ACCOUNT (PA, Sim Funded) ---
- Safety Net = start + drawdown + $100; once EOD balance exceeds it, floor locks
  permanently and full contracts unlock. Before that: half-max contracts only.
- Payout consistency: no single day above 50% of total profit at request (loosened
  from 30%). No chart screenshots / manual trade reviews (removed Mar 2026).
- Minimum 8 trading days between payout requests; at least 5 profitable days $50+ each.
- Minimum payout $500. First 5 payouts capped per size; from 6th onward withdraw full available.
- Prohibited on PA: full automation/bots/EAs (eval-only), HFT, DCA on losers, hedging
  during news (both-side news gambling), unapproved automation. Semi-automated
  (monitored) only per Mar-2026 automation policy.
- Positions must be flat before market-close cutoff. Normal news trading allowed.
- MAE rule and 5:1 risk-reward rule REMOVED Mar 2026 (legacy accounts still bound).
