SECTION 5 — MODEL-BY-MODEL RULES
================================================================================
--- 5A. HYPER GROWTH (1-step, $5K/$10K/$20K+, 1:30) ---
- Profit target: 10%, one step, eval only. Funded: each 10% = DOUBLE account.
- Max loss: 6% stop-out, eval + every funded level. ($10K: $600 room; floor
  $9,400. $20K: $19, - wait: $20,000 − $1,200 = $18,800 floor.)
- Daily loss: 3% PAUSE — trading halted for the day, account NOT terminated.
  ($10K: $300/day. $20K: $600/day.)
- Scaling: every 10% funded profit doubles account to $4M cap; split rises
  50% → 100% along the way.
- DD calc + example ($10K): start $10,000, stop $9,400. Day opens $10,300 →
  pause line $10,300 − $300 = $10,000 intraday. Close $11,000 (+10%) → funded
  $10K; next +$1,000 (10%) → $20K account.
--- 5B. PRO GROWTH (1-step, $5K–$50K, 1:30) ---
- Profit target: 10%, one step. Same 10%/6%/1:30 as Hyper Growth BUT:
  (a) 3% daily TERMINATES (no pause), (b) 3 minimum profitable days required,
  (c) split starts 75/25 (vs 50/50), (d) scaling INCREMENTAL ($5K→$7.5K→$10K…)
  to $500K cap (vs doubling to $4M).
- DD calc + example ($10K Pro): start $10,000, stop $9,400. A single −$300
  intraday = dead (vs Hyper pause). Must print 3 green days before pass.
--- 5C. HIGH STAKES (2-step, $2.5K–$100K, 1:100) ---
- Profit target: 10% Step 1 + 5% Step 2, eval only. ($50K: $5,000+$2,500.
  $100K: $10,000+$5,000.)
- Max loss: 10% STATIC floor, never moves, eval + funded. ($100K: $90,000.
  $25K: $22,500.)
- Daily loss: 5%. ($100K: $5,000/day. $25K: $1,250/day.)
- Split: starts 80/20 → up to 100% with scale; $500K level adds $10,000 fixed
  payout on top. Typical funded 80%, bi-weekly.
- DD calc + example ($25K): start $25,000, floor $22,500. Step 1 target
  $27,500. Day opens $26,000 → daily limit $24,750. Cushion built early is
  permanent (static never trails against you).
--- 5D. BOOTCAMP (3-step, $6K/$20K/$100K/$250K, 1:30) ---
- Profit target: 6% per step × 3 steps (low per-step ask, long road).
  ($20K: $1,200 ×3. $100K: $6,000 ×3.)
- Max loss: 5% per step during challenge; 4% once FUNDED (tightens!).
  ($100K: $5,000/step room; funded $4,000 room. $250K: $12,500/step.)
- Daily loss: 3% pause at funded stage.
- Split: starts 50/50 → up to 100%; scales every 5% funded profit to $4M cap.
- Accounts: up to 4 concurrent Bootcamp accounts across sizes.
- DD calc + example ($20K): start $20,000, step room $1,000. Pass 3 × $1,200
  → funded $20K with $800 (4%) room — trade smaller than eval.
