--- 5C. BUY-NOW-PAY-LATER (deferred-fee crypto eval) ---
- Evaluation: 4% target, 8% STATIC EOD max drawdown, NO daily limit, NO min
  days, NO consistency, news/volatility trading fully permitted. Small
  upfront fee ($5-$20 by size); pay the remainder ($69-$589) ONLY after
  passing to activate funded.
- Funded stage (rules tighten at activation): 3% daily EOD limit (00:00 UTC
  reset); max drawdown switches to 5% TRAILING on highest EOD equity
  (official: $10K funded starts at $9,500 breach level; EOD equity $11,000
  => new level $10,450); 20% consistency score required before EACH
  withdrawal (biggest day / total profit x 100 ≤ 20%); WITHDRAW LOCK —
  generated profit raises the trailing floor (earn $1,000 => floor +$1,000;
  withdraw $500 => floor stays +$500 above start).
- Funded split 100% (per third-party reads of official pages).
--- 5D. GENESIS (COMING SOON — not purchasable) ---
- Withdraw-any-day ladder: request once per day whenever ahead.
- Winning day = closed-day gain >1% of initial (00:00-23:59 UTC) => +1 level;
  losing day (<-1%) => -1 level (floor level 1); neutral otherwise.
- Split ladder 14 levels: 5/7/10/14/19/25/32/40/49/59/70/82/95/109%. Net
  levels = winning days minus losing days. Withdraw resets to level 1 and
  initial balance. Fee refunded on reaching level 10.
- Drawdown: 4% EOD-trailing (official example: EOD equity $105,000 =>
  breach $100,800).
--- 5E. NEWS / WEEKEND / INACTIVITY (ALL TRACKS) ---
- News: fully permitted, evaluation AND funded — no blackout windows.
- Weekend/overnight: permitted on all account types.
- Inactivity: trade ≥ once per 28 days (FAQ) / 30 days (Terms).
