--- 5A. ALPHA ONE (1-step, 6%/10%/12% variants, 1 min day) ---
- Profit target: variant % once (6/10/12). ($100K One-10%: $10,000.)
- Max loss: 6% TRAILING high-water-mark; locks at starting balance once up
  6%. ($100K: $6,000 trail; at $106,000 floor = $100,000 forever.)
- Daily loss: 4% (higher of day-start balance vs equity). ($100K: $4,000.)
- Min days: 1. Fastest path. On-demand payouts only. Weekend hold on
  Qualified: ALLOWED (unlike Pro).
- DD calc + example ($100K One-10%): start $100,000, floor trails $94,000→.
  Peak $103,000 → floor $97,000. Hit $106,000 → floor $100,000 locked.
  Day opens $102,000 bal / $101,800 eq → daily base $102,000 → $97,920 limit.
--- 5B. ALPHA PRO (2-step, 6%/8%/10% variants, highest FX leverage) ---
- Targets: Pro-8%: 8%/5%; Pro-10%: 10%/5%; Pro-6%: low-target variant.
  ($100K Pro-10%: $10,000+$5,000.)
- Max loss STATIC: 6%/8%/10% by variant. ($100K Pro-10%: $90,000 flat.)
- Daily: Pro-6% 3% (higher of balance/equity); Pro-8% 4% balance-based;
  Pro-10% 5% balance-based. ($100K Pro-10%: $5,000/day.)
- Min days: 3 per phase. Weekend: eval ALLOWED; Qualified NOT allowed.
- News: 5-min blackout each side on targeted instrument (all Pro).
- Payouts: bi-weekly OR on-demand (exclusive); 90% add-on ONLY on on-demand
  Pro at purchase (~+10% price).
- DD calc + example ($50K Pro-8%): start $50,000, floor $46,000 static.
  Ph1 $4,000 → Ph2 $2,500. Day opens $52,000 balance → daily limit $49,920
  (4% balance-based).
--- 5C. ALPHA SWING (2-step long-hold, on-demand only) ---
- Targets 10%/5%; Max 10% static; Daily 5% balance-based; 3 days/phase.
  ($100K: $10K+$5K targets; $90K floor; $5K/day.)
- Weekend hold: ALLOWED evaluation + Qualified (core feature).
- News: trades opened within 2 min either side (4-min window) must last
  >2 minutes duration (Swing-specific rule).
- Leverage lowest FX room (1:30) + half lot caps ($100K: 20 lots).
- Gambling policy + Focused Trader Group rules bind Swing.
- DD calc + example ($100K Swing): start $100,000 floor $90,000. Hold
  weekend gap-up to $108,000 → Phase 1 done with 3+ days; floor still
  $90,000 static.
--- 5D. ALPHA THREE (3-step, 10K–200K, lower per-phase targets) ---
- Targets 8%/4%/4% in order; Max 6% static; Daily 4% (higher of EOD balance
  or equity); 3 days/phase. ($100K: $8K/$4K/$4K; $94K floor; $4K/day.)
- Weekend hold ALLOWED all stages. News 5-min blackout. Leverage FX 1:50.
- Payouts bi-weekly OR on-demand. No $5K size.
- DD calc + example ($50K Three): start $50,000 floor $47,000. Clear $4,000
  → $2,000 → $2,000 across 9+ min days; floor never moves.
--- 5E. ALPHA DIRECT (instant-Qualified, no eval) ---
- Start Qualified day one; 90% split STANDARD; on-demand under Direct rules;
  counts to $400K allocation. Read /resources/instant-funding-is-here page
  for Direct DD/payout fine print before buying.
--- 5F. NEWS / WEEKEND / OVERNIGHT (cross-model) ---
- Eval: news trading UNRESTRICTED all plans.
- Qualified: Pro-8/10 within 2 min = restricted; Pro-6/One/Three within
  5 min = restricted; Swing 2-min-open → 2-min-min-duration rule.
- Weekend Qualified: Pro NOT allowed; One/Three/Swing ALLOWED.
