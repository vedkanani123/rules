--- 5A. STELLAR 2-STEP (CFD, flagship) ---
- Profit target: 8% Phase 1, 5% Phase 2, NONE on FundedNext Account. Closed
  trades only. ($100K: $8,000 then $5,000. $6K: $480 then $300.)

  unrealized P/L + swap + commission. Breach = account closed. ($100K: $5,000
  per day. $6K: $300 per day.)
- Maximum Loss Limit: 10% of INITIAL balance, STATIC (fixed from starting
  balance, never trails). Breach = fail. ($100K: cannot fall below $90,000.
  $6K: cannot fall below $5,400.)
- Minimum trading days: 5 per phase (min 1 trade each day); none on funded.
  No time limit either phase. No-min-days Add-On removes this rule.

  news-trading profit counts toward targets/withdrawals.
- First reward: 21 days after FundedNext Account activation, then every
  14 days (Bi-Weekly Add-On: every 14 days from the start, skipping the
  initial 21-day cycle). 80% standard share; 150%-refund add-on available.
- Fee refund: full challenge fee refunded WITH 1ST reward (fastest refund of
  all Stellar plans; "fee-back reward"). Example: $100K fee back with first
  payout on request.
- Challenge reward: 15% of challenge-phase profits paid after first scale-up.

  ($100K: $10,000. $6K: $600.)

  commission). Breach = closed. ($100K: $3,000/day; floor $97,000 Day 1.)
