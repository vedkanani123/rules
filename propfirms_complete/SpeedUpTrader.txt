- Consistency: profits must show regular pattern; single-day windfall vs
  average-day ratio reviewed (cannot let one day dominate and coast).

- Contracts: 3 / 6 / 9 max open across all products.
- Session: flat by 4:10 PM ET daily; no overnight holds.
- News: may not trade through major news events (restricted windows).
- Funded trades under 30 seconds flagged/closed per funded terms (scalp rule).
--- 5B. FUNDED ACCOUNT ---
- Step-up fee ~$100 after pass (legacy $200+$100 refs; current checkout governs).
- Drawdown: trailing per size, locks once buffered; same daily loss rule.
- Weekly activity: 1+ trade per week minimum.
- News/flat rules carry; 30-second minimum hold on funded.
- No monthly fee once funded (test fee only + step-up).
--- 5C. COMBINE NOTES ---
- One Rithmic/CQG identity; multiple tests allowed via separate purchases.
- Offshore-broker structure; not NFA/CFTC member, not a broker.
