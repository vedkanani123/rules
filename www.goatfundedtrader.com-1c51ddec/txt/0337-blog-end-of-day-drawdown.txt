The next day's limit changes based on the previous day's closing balance, not the intraday high. This overnight adjustment gives you a steady reference point throughout the trading day.What flexibility does this give your trading strategyYou can take a position that temporarily drops your account below your starting balance without breaking your drawdown limit, provided you close the day within acceptable limits.
