Readings above 70 point to overbought conditions and readings below 30 point to oversold conditions, though aggressive intraday traders often tighten or widen those thresholds. J. Welles Wilder Jr. introduced the indicator in 1978, and traders still rely on its simple numeric readout to judge the speed and direction of price moves.How RSI Is Calculated: The Numbers Behind the LineRSI starts with average gains and average losses over a chosen period. Calculate RS as average gain divided by average loss, then RSI equals 100 minus 100 divided by one plus RS. The default period is 14, using Wilder smoothing, but you can change the period to change sensitivity.
