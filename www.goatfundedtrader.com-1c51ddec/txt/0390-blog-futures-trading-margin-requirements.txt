This deposit remains yours throughout the trade, held by the clearinghouse as a performance bond that guarantees you'll fulfill the contract terms regardless of market direction. Unlike stock margin accounts that charge interest on borrowed funds, futures margin involves zero borrowing and zero interest, making it a fundamentally different mechanism built around risk coverage rather than debt.Leveraged ExposureThe leverage this structure unlocks is substantial. Positive adjustments boost available capital for withdrawals or new trades, while negative ones reduce equity and can trigger margin deficiencies that demand immediate attention.
