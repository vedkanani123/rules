The exchange's matching engine searches the order book and pairs your request with someone willing to take the opposite side. Once matched, the system transfers the cryptocurrency to your wallet and debits your account simultaneously. This happens 24/7 in crypto markets, driven by live supply and demand with no middleman slowing settlement.Why Spot Trading Differs From DerivativesSpot trading gives you the actual asset, not a contract predicting its future value. Spot keeps outcomes tied directly to the asset's performance: if Bitcoin rises 10%, your holdings rise 10%. If it falls, you still own the coins and can wait for recovery.
