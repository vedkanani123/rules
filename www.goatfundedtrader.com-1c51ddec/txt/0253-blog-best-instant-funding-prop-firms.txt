How Instant Funding Works, and What It Costs You
The trade is simple. A prop firm without evaluation flips the usual arrangement.

Evaluations cost less upfront and demand you hit a profit target first.
Instant funding costs a bit more at checkout and hands you the account immediately.

Three instant funding drawdown rules and related structures decide how well an account actually treats you.

Drawdown type: A static floor sits fixed at your starting balance and stays there permanently. In contrast, a trailing floor climbs as your equity climbs, which protects gains while shrinking your room during a pullback. The majority of instant accounts run trailing, so understanding the version applied to your specific plan is important before trades are placed.

Consistency rules: 20% consistency rule means a single day cannot supply 20% or more of your profit across the payout period. Catch one clean move on a central bank announcement, and your payout gets delayed while you grind out additional sessions to balance the ledger.

Floating loss rules: Some firms close an account permanently the moment unrealised losses cross a threshold, independent of your drawdown figure. Position sizing has to account for it from trade one.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Can I hold several instant funded accounts with Goat Funded Trader?
GFT allows traders to purchase multiple challenges and accounts under a single registered account. A trader cannot create multiple registered GFT profiles. GFT also applies rules around trading across multiple accounts, including restrictions on using accounts to exploit market conditions or the firm. For Instant Funding accounts specifically, eligible accounts can be merged when they are new or have been freshly reset after a reward.
Does a Goat Funded Trader instant funded account expire if I stop trading?
GFT's standard inactivity rule applies to simulated funded accounts: an account is breached if no trade is placed for 30 consecutive days. The account cannot be reinstated after an inactivity breach, and the fee is not refunded. Placing at least one trade within every 30 days keeps the account active.
Is the instant funding fee refundable at Goat Funded Trader?
Yes, GFT's current policy states that the evaluation fee is refundable when the account reaches its fourth payout, including Instant Funding accounts. If the account is breached before reaching the fourth payout, the fee is not refundable.

KYC is completed when requesting the first payout. GFT uses crypto, Skrill, and bank-transfer payouts, while Rise handles verification for Rise payouts.
