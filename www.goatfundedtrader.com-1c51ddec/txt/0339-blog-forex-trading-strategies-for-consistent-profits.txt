Trend Following StrategyTrend following captures sustained directional moves by aligning with momentum rather than predicting reversals. Identify the primary trend using moving averages on daily or weekly charts, enter on pullbacks to support or resistance within that trend, and hold until clear reversal signals appear. This method works best during strong economic cycles, when differences in central bank policy or sustained growth disparities push currency pairs in one direction for weeks or months.When should you deploy trend following strategies?Use this strategy when major currency pairs like EUR/USD or GBP/USD show a clear pattern of higher highs and higher lows on daily charts, particularly after policy announcements that reinforce the prevailing trend. For long positions, place your stop loss below the recent swing low; for short positions, place it above the swing high. Aim for profits several times larger than your initial risk. This method suits patient traders who prefer fewer trades, tolerate small pullbacks without panic, and cannot monitor markets constantly.2. Day Trading StrategyDay trading captures intraday swings by opening and closing all positions before the session ends, avoiding overnight gaps from news surprises. Analyze 15-minute to 1-hour charts for support and resistance breaks or candlestick patterns, keeping to three to five trades per day with predefined entry rules and daily loss limits. This approach works during high-volume sessions with clear directional bias, especially following central bank announcements or economic data releases that create sustained intraday momentum.When should you use Forex trading strategies for consistent profits through day trading?Use this when you can watch markets closely during specific hours but want no exposure to after-hours events that could gap against your positions.
