Founded in 2012, Topstep helps traders develop their skills and achieve consistent profitability without risking their capital. The firm built its reputation by offering traders a structured path to trading live capital through an innovative evaluation program called the Trading Combine. This program simulates real-market conditions, allowing traders to demonstrate their ability to manage risk and generate profits before being granted access to a funded trading account. Topstep’s mission centers on fostering disciplined trading habits and providing a supportive community to help traders grow professionally. Why is Topstep Unique?Topstep’s Trading Combine is a unique, one-step evaluation process where traders must reach specific profit targets while adhering to strict rules on daily losses and maximum drawdowns. This ensures that only those who can trade consistently and responsibly qualify for funding. Upon successful completion, traders receive an Express Funded Account with preset buying power, which varies by account tier: $50,000$100,000$150,000The program’s design encourages traders to develop strong risk management skills and discipline, which are crucial for long-term success in the futures markets. How Much Can I Make With Topstep? Topstep’s payout structure is designed to reward traders generously while maintaining a sustainable business model. Traders keep 100% of their profits up to the first $10,000 earned, after which a 90/10 profit split applies, with the majority of profits going to the trader. This payout policy incentivizes traders to perform well and provides a clear financial reward for their efforts. Traders can request payouts up to four times a month, with payments processed quickly via ACH or international wire transfers. This flexibility and transparency in profit sharing make Topstep an attractive option for aspiring futures traders. What Features Does Topstep Offer? Beyond funding, Topstep offers an advanced trading platform called TopstepX. This platform integrates powerful charting tools, real-time market sentiment indicators, and live broadcasts to inform and motivate traders. The platform supports trading popular futures contracts, including: IndicesCommoditiesMicro contractsIt also caters to various trading styles and preferences. Topstep also provides extensive educational resources and a trader community, reinforcing its commitment to helping traders improve continuously and achieve sustainable success in the competitive futures trading landscape.What Is Apex Trader Funding?Apex Trader Funding is a proprietary trading firm based in Austin, Texas, specializing exclusively in futures markets. Founded in 2021, the company was created to enhance the trader funding model by offering more competitive payouts and a more trader-friendly approach than existing firms. Apex focuses on futures trading, which involves contracts to buy or sell commodities at predetermined prices on future dates. It is known for its high liquidity and ability to leverage prominent positions with relatively small capital. The firm has rapidly grown into a global community with tens of thousands of members spanning over 150 countries, establishing itself as a leading player in the futures prop trading space.The Evaluation Process at Apex Trader FundingThe evaluation process at Apex Trader Funding is notably straightforward, consisting of just one step. Traders who pass this evaluation receive an initial funded account of $25,000, with no profit sharing, meaning they keep 100% of their gains up to that amount. Beyond the initial $25,000 profit, a profit split of 90% to the trader and 10% to Apex applies. Traders can scale their accounts up to a maximum balance of $300,000. This structure is designed to reward successful traders generously while providing a clear path to scaling capital. Apex allows traders to request two monthly profit payouts, which is more frequent than many competitors.Risk Management and Trading Flexibility at Apex Trader FundingOne of Apex Trader Funding's key advantages is its risk management system, which automatically caps position sizes to prevent traders from exceeding their limits and risking account termination. This contrasts with other prop firms, where breaching position limits can result in immediate account loss. The firm also imposes no daily drawdown limits, which provides traders with more flexibility and less pressure to meet strict daily loss thresholds. Apex supports multiple trading platforms, such as Rithmic/NinjaTrader and Tradovate/NinjaTrader. It offers tools such as trade copiers for managing multiple accounts and algorithmic trading, appealing to various trading styles and strategies.Pricing, Fees, and Payouts at Apex Trader FundingApex Trader Funding’s subscription fees vary depending on the account size and include necessary data feeds and platform costs. The firm prides itself on quick payouts, responsive customer support, and continuous improvements to its trading environment. Traders can trade during holidays and news events within specified hours, and the evaluation period requires as little as seven trading days to qualify. Apex stands out by permitting traders to keep all profits up to $25,000, significantly higher than Topstep’s threshold of $10,000. This means that Apex traders can enjoy a longer period of full profit retention before entering the profit-sharing phase, potentially increasing their earnings without initially sharing them with the firm. After reaching these thresholds, both firms apply a profit split, where traders receive 90% of subsequent profits, while the firm retains 10%. This 90/10 split is quite favorable compared to many other proprietary trading firms, making Apex and Topstep attractive for traders seeking to maximize their income. Apex’s higher initial full-retention amount can be decisive for traders aiming for larger upfront gains before profit sharing begins. CostApex Trader Funding offers a straightforward pricing model with two primary options: monthly or one-time lifetime fees, which vary based on account size and trading platform. Monthly fees typically range from $85 to $105, while lifetime fees can be between $130 and $360. This flexibility enables traders to select a payment plan that best suits their trading style and financial situation, thereby providing greater control over their costs. In contrast, Topstep’s pricing structure is more layered and complex. It includes a monthly Trading Combine fee of $49 for a $50,000 account, a one-time activation fee of $149, additional costs for advanced market data at $39 per month, and platform fees. Unlike Apex, Topstep does not offer a lifetime fee option, which can lead to higher cumulative costs over time. The multiple fees and ongoing costs may be a consideration for traders who prefer a more straightforward and potentially more affordable fee structure. FlexibilityRegarding tradable assets, Apex and Topstep cover common futures markets such as: EquityForexAgriculturalInterest rateMetals futuresApex extends its offerings to include energy futures, cryptocurrency futures, micro-futures, EUREX instruments, and even U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement instruments, providing a broader range of trading opportunities. Topstep includes oil, natural gas, and some financial futures, but its asset variety is somewhat narrower than Apex's. Apex also offers more flexibility in trading style and scaling. Scaling Freedom and Trading Flexibility: Key Differences Between Apex and Topstep ModelsTraders can follow various scaling plans that reward consistent profits without a maximum earning cap, as long as they adhere to Apex’s evaluation rules. Topstep employs an incremental growth model tied to its evaluation process, allowing traders to increase their position sizes as their performance improves gradually. Topstep enforces a “day trading only” policy, restricting trades to short-term positions, while Apex places no such time-of-day limitations and offers traders the freedom to trade whenever they prefer. Risk ManagementApex provides traders with greater autonomy in managing risk, enabling them to set personalized risk parameters based on their tolerance levels. This flexibility empowers traders to tailor their strategies and risk controls to their individual preferences. Topstep enforces stricter risk management rules, including specific daily loss limits and maximum position sizes, which are designed to protect both the trader and the firm from excessive losses. Risk Management and Profit Targets: A Closer Look at Apex vs TopstepBoth firms impose limits on daily and overall drawdowns to safeguard against significant losses within a single day or cumulatively. Apex rewards traders who reach profit milestones while adhering to risk limits, encouraging disciplined trading. Topstep’s profit targets and risk controls are closely linked, requiring traders to remain within prescribed risk parameters to maintain their funded status. This structured approach instills discipline but can feel restrictive compared to Apex’s more flexible policies. Educational ResourcesBoth Apex and Topstep offer comprehensive educational resources to support trader development. These include trading courses, webinars, strategy guides, and active trader communities informing participants about market trends and trading techniques. Such resources are valuable for traders at all levels seeking to improve their skills and stay updated on industry developments. Apex supplements its educational offerings with personalized one-on-one coaching from experienced mentors, providing tailored guidance and support. Topstep, meanwhile, offers a more comprehensive range of educational materials and structured performance reviews, designed to help traders refine their strategies systematically. This difference means Apex may appeal more to traders who prefer individualized mentorship, while Topstep caters to those who benefit from a broader range of structured learning tools. Trading PlatformApex and Topstep support a variety of popular trading platforms, including: ATASBookmapJigsawMotiveWaveNinjaTraderQuantowerSierra ChartThis wide platform support ensures traders can use familiar and preferred tools to execute their strategies effectively. Topstep additionally offers its proprietary platform, TopstepX, which is designed to provide an integrated trading experience tailored to its funded traders. Apex does not have a proprietary platform but compensates with extensive platform compatibility. This difference may influence traders who prefer a proprietary solution with specialized features versus those who value flexibility in choosing their trading software.
