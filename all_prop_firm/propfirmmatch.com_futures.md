## Homepage FAQ (Futures)

### What are futures prop firms?

Futures prop firms are prop trading firms that give traders access to futures trading evaluations, usually through accounts with predefined rules and risk limits. Traders typically pay an evaluation fee, follow the firm's trading rules, and may become eligible for payouts if they meet the firm's requirements.

These firms are different from futures brokers. A futures prop firm usually provides an evaluation or funded-style trading program, while a broker provides direct market access and handles customer trading accounts. Traders can view [vetted futures firms](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/all-prop-firms?tab=all) on the Prop Firm Match Futures page.

### How do futures prop firms work?

Futures prop firms usually require traders to complete an evaluation before becoming eligible for a funded or payout-eligible account. The trader must meet a profit target while following rules such as drawdown limits, daily loss limits, position size limits, consistency rules, and minimum trading days.

If the trader passes the evaluation, the firm may provide access to a funded account. Payout eligibility then depends on the firm's profit split, payout schedule, trading rules, and account terms. Traders can compare account structures, rules, platforms, and reviews on the Prop Firm Match [Futures comparison page](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-challenges).

### Are futures prop firms safe?

Futures prop firms vary in transparency, rules, payout history, and business practices. A safer evaluation process starts with reviewing whether the firm clearly explains its account model, drawdown rules, payout conditions, fees, prohibited strategies, and trader agreement.

Before joining a futures prop firm, traders should review [verified trader feedback](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-reviews#table-scroll-target), payout terms, [rule changes](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-rules), support quality, and whether the firm's terms are clear. Prop Firm Match lists vetted futures prop firms and helps traders compare them side by side using firm data, reviews, rules, and platform information.

### Are futures prop firms regulated?

Most online futures prop firms are not regulated in the same way as futures brokers or futures commission merchants. Many operate evaluation-based or simulated trading models rather than directly handling customer futures accounts.

Prop Firm Match applies a strict vetting process before listing firms on the platform, but traders should still review each firm's rules, disclosures, and account terms to understand how the firm operates.

### Why do some futures prop firms charge monthly fees?

Some futures prop firms charge monthly fees to provide access to evaluation accounts, trading platforms, market data, account monitoring, support, and risk-management systems. In many cases, the fee continues until the trader passes, cancels, or resets the evaluation.

Although some firms use monthly subscriptions, others only have one-time fees, activation fees, or reset fees. Traders can use the Prop Firm Match Futures [comparison page](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-challenges) to compare firms by account details, offers, platforms, rules, and other decision-making criteria.

### Which prop firms offer futures trading?

Several prop firms offer futures trading evaluations or funded-style accounts. Prop Firm Match maintains a dedicated [Futures Prop Firms](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/all-prop-firms?tab=all) list where traders can compare futures firms by account size, platform, trading rules, payout terms, user reviews, discounts, and other key criteria.

Because firm rules, supported platforms, and offers can change, traders should review the latest terms before purchasing an evaluation. Comparing firms side by side can help traders find a futures prop firm that better matches their trading style, risk tolerance, and platform preference.

### What trading platforms do futures prop firms use?

Futures prop firms may support platforms such as Project X, TradingView, NinjaTrader, Tradovate, Rithmic, and other futures trading platforms. Availability can vary by firm, account type, broker connection, data provider, and region, so traders should confirm platform support before purchasing an evaluation.

Prop Firm Match provides platform-based comparison pages to help traders find futures prop firms by preferred trading platform, including [TradingView](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-lists/platforms/tradingview), [NinjaTrader](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-lists/platforms/ninjatrader), [Tradovate](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-lists/platforms/tradovate), and [ATAS Orderflow Trading](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-lists/platforms/atas-orderflow-trading) where available.

### What are the best futures prop firms?

The best futures prop firm depends on the trader's strategy, budget, preferred platform, risk tolerance, and payout goals. A firm with a low evaluation fee may not be the best fit if its drawdown rules, payout conditions, or platform setup do not match the trader's approach.

When comparing futures prop firms, traders should review evaluation costs, drawdown type, profit target, payout schedule, platform availability, supported contracts, scaling rules, and verified reviews. Traders can use the Prop Firm Match [Features page](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-lists) to explore these criteria by category, including platforms, challenges, payout methods, brokers, assets, and account features.

### How much do futures prop firms pay?

Futures prop firm payouts depend on the firm's profit split, account type, payout schedule, and trader performance. Many firms offer traders a percentage of eligible profits, but payout rules vary widely between firms.

Before choosing a futures prop firm, traders should review the profit split, minimum payout threshold, payout frequency, consistency rules, withdrawal limits, and any conditions that may affect payout eligibility. Traders can compare payout-related criteria on the [Prop Firm Match Payouts page](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/prop-firm-lists/payouts), including profit split programs, payout frequency, withdrawal methods, and consistency-rule payout programs.

### What is the maximum drawdown in a futures prop firm account?

The maximum drawdown in a futures prop firm account is the largest loss a trader can reach before violating the firm's rules. It may be calculated as a static drawdown, trailing drawdown, end-of-day drawdown, or intraday drawdown, depending on the firm.

Drawdown rules can significantly affect how a trader manages risk. Traders can read [Prop Firm Match's drawdown limitations guide](https://propfirmmatch.com/futures/blog/understanding-drawdown-limitations-in-prop-trading-risks-and-management) to understand how different drawdown types work, then compare rule-related criteria across firms on the Futures comparison page.
