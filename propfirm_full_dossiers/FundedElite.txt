--- 5A. 2-STEP CHALLENGE (standard + Free Retry) ---

  to 50% permanently.
--- 5B. 1-STEP CHALLENGE (standard + Free Retry) ---

- Daily loss: 3%. Max loss: 6-8% static eval, trailing on funded.

  (must select + price in at setup). Trades held <3 min flagged (<30s with add-on).
- Same Free Retry 50%-split penalty as 2-Step.
--- 5C. LITE 2-STEP / LITE 1-STEP ---
- Budget versions from $19 (Lite 2-Step 5K) / $24 (Lite 1-Step 5K). Same phase
  structure as standard but tighter drawdowns on some configs (5-6%).
- No free retries on Lite. Same news/weekend freedom, same funded trailing switch.
--- 5D. INSTANT FUNDING ---
- No evaluation. Sizes 7K-100K, fees $159-$1,489. Daily 3% trailing, max 6%
  trailing from day one. No profit target. Requires 3 profitable days (0.5%+ each).
- Split: first payout ALWAYS 70% regardless of selection; chosen split
  (60/70/80/95%) applies from second payout onward.
- First payout after 21 days regardless of selected interval. Consistency 25%.
--- 5E. FLASH ACTIVATION ---
- $5 entry for 25K-200K. One-step 6% target, 3% daily, 6% static max. Minimum
  1 trading day eval + 3 profitable live days. Activation fee after passing:
  $159 (25K) / $329 (50K) / $499 (100K) / $989 (200K), payable within 29 days
  or pass expires. Default split 80%, selectable 60-95%. Default payout 14 days.
--- 5F. CUSTOM CHALLENGE + CATALYST ---
- Custom: set own target, drawdown (5-10%), split (60-95%), payout cycle
  (3/7/14/21 days). Price varies with risk selected (riskier config = cheaper).
- Catalyst ladder: staged growth to ~$1M reported; 3% daily vs last EOD equity/
  balance; trailing max 6% at 25K, 5% above; 5 profitable days per stage with
  minimum PnL ($150 at 25K rising to $1,000+ at top); splits 80/85/90/95% by size.
--- 5G. SHARED RULES (all models) ---
- Single-instrument risk cap: may not risk >50% of daily loss on one instrument
  in one day (e.g. 2.5% on 5% daily). Risk counts even on winners; no stop =
  max adverse excursion; with stop = WIDEST stop ever set during trade life.
- EAs allowed as analytical/risk tools; fully-automated hidden/HFT EAs banned;
  passing with hidden/HFT EAs demotes funded account back to evaluation.
- Copy trading own accounts allowed; third-party/cross-account copying banned.
- HFT/tick-scalping (<3 min, <30s with add-on), latency arbitrage, cross-broker
  hedging, server spamming, account rolling banned. Martingale/grid tolerated
  if non-exploitative. Account sharing: single consistent IP only.

7. Account rolling / gambling / risk-free arbitrage across firms.
8. KYC fraud / false identity; chargeback abuse.
RISK RULES: stops required discipline; martingale/grid tolerated only if
non-exploitative and within single-instrument cap. Scalping must be pre-selected.
IP CONSISTENCY: single consistent IP required; Tradezella-type tools have
triggered flags (resolve via support before payout).
Consequences ladder: profit-split reduction (to 50% or 30%), payout interview
failure = settlement offer, suspension, termination.
