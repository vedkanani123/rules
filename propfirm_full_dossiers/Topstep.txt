SECTION 5 — MODEL-BY-MODEL RULES
================================================================================
--- 5A. TRADING COMBINE (Evaluation, 50K/100K/150K) ---
- Profit Target: $3,000 (50K) / $6,000 (100K) / $9,000 (150K) net, positions
  closed. Must be reached without any rule breach.
- Maximum Loss Limit (THE One Rule): EOD-TRAILING. Formula: account high minus
  drawdown amount = floor, recalculated at end of each day. Initial floors:
  $48,000 / $97,000 / $145,500. Floor moves UP only on closed EOD balance,
  never down. Intraday open profits do NOT move the floor. Breach is checked
  in REAL TIME including unrealized P/L — touching the floor = fail even
  though the floor only steps at EOD.
  Example ($50K): start $50K floor $48K; close $54K -> floor $52K; dip to
  $51,900 intraday -> FAILED despite prior profit.
- Daily Loss Limit (optional, added at checkout): $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000.
  Safety net, NOT a breach — platform blocks further trades until next market
  open. One bad day cannot blow the account if DLL enabled.
- Consistency Target: best single day must stay <=50% of Profit Target
  ($1,500 / $3,000 / $4,500). If exceeded, Consistency Target increases
  (must grow total to rebalance). No minimum days beyond 2.
- Max Contracts: 5 / 10 / 15 minis (50 / 100 / 150 micros), shared pool.
  Holding more than allowed = breach.
- News/weekend: no blackout; trade through news, hold overnight per CME hours.
  No intraday trailing beyond MLL mechanics.
- Reset: $49 / $99 / $199 per reset; one free Reset Credit on each rebill.
--- 5B. EXPRESS FUNDED ACCOUNT (XFA, simulated funded) ---
- Activation: $149 on Standard Path; $0 on No-Activation path. Paid once per
  XFA after Combine pass.
- MLL: same EOD-trailing engine, LOCKS at $0 (breakeven) once reached.
- Two payout-eligibility paths (since Feb 5, 2026):
  Standard: minimum 5 winning days of $150+ net each; maintain balance between
    payouts; no cumulative profit minimum (removed Apr 28, 2026).
  Consistency: minimum 3 trading days (1+ trade each), best day <=40% of total
    profit; no cumulative minimum.
- Payout request cap per request (since Apr 28, 2026): capped at 50% of
  account balance, up to $2K/$3K/$5K Standard or $3K/$4K/$6K Consistency by
  size (50K/100K/150K). With Responsible Trading DLL at checkout: DOUBLE caps
  ($4K/$6K/$10K-class and $6K/$8K/$12K-class; 150K Consistency $12K max).
- Back2Funded: blown XFA can be reactivated to continue without new Combine.
- Breach of MLL on XFA = loss of that XFA (Back2Funded available).
--- 5C. LIVE FUNDED ACCOUNT (LFA, real capital, invite only) ---
- Invite only at TopstepFunded discretion; 0.71% of XFA traders called up
  in 2025. No purchase path. Separate Live Funded Account Rules apply.
- Remaining on XFA indefinitely with payouts is normal; do not buy a Combine
  expecting a Live invite.
--- 5D. SESSION / OPERATIONAL NOTES ---
- Trading day boundary follows CME session; MLL steps at EOD close.
- VPN hard-banned per tester reports; copy-trading others' signals as
  decision-maker risks closure (see Section 8). Automation via ProjectX
