SECTION 5 — MODEL-BY-MODEL RULES
================================================================================
--- 5A. EVALUATION (all sizes) ---
- Targets/DD per table; trailing STOPS at initial starting balance (floor never
  rises above start — most forgiving trailing variant).
- 80% consistency: sum of 3 other best days must be >=80% of best day.
  Example: best $1,000 -> need $800 combined from next 3 ($400+$300+$240=$940
  PASS). Completable in 2 days; not a breach, blocks placement until balanced.
- Trading day: 5:00 PM CT to 3:15 PM CT next day. NO opens/holds 3:15-5:00 PM CT;
  all positions MUST be closed by 3:15 PM CT.
- Contracts: hard max per size across all products simultaneously.
- Pass: hit target + min days + consistency + no DD breach -> email evaluation
  team; review <=3 days; partner contract + funded credentials.
--- 5B. EXPRESS EVALUATION ---
- Same rules/targets; 5-day minimum instead of 10.
--- 5C. FUNDED (FUNDING PARTNER) ---
- Daily loss limit, max trailing, position-size rules per partner contract.
- First $10,000 of withdrawals FREE (100%), then 90/10.
- Free unlimited withdrawals from day 1; 2-strike policy when funded.
- Partners cover data fees; trader focuses on execution.
