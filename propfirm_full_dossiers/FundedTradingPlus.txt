SECTION 5 — MODEL-BY-MODEL RULES
================================================================================
--- 5A. 2-STEP CLASSIC (CFD, 5K-200K) ---
- Profit targets: 7% Phase 1 + 7% Phase 2 (e.g. $100K: +$7,000 then +$7,000).
- Daily Loss Limit: 4-6% depending on size/variant (e.g. ~5% on $100K).
  Resets daily; equity-based (balance + open P/L - commissions/swaps).
- Maximum Loss: 8% STATIC on classic variants / 6% TRAILING on select
  variants (LuneFi: "6% trailing or 8% static"). Confirm variant card.
- Consistency: 35% during BOTH challenge phases + 50% on the simulated live
  account (Help Center per Fortunly). A single day may not exceed 35% (eval)
  / 50% (live) of total profit at payout/checkpoint — keep trading till
  ratio is fixed; not an auto-breach.
- Leverage up to 1:50. First reward after 10 days, then every 10 days.
--- 5B. 1-STEP EXPRESS (CFD, 5K-200K) ---
- Profit target: 10% single phase (e.g. $100K: +$10,000). None on funded.
- Daily Loss Limit: 4%. Maximum Loss: 6% TRAILING.
- Consistency: 35% on select evaluations (LuneFi matrix); confirm variant.
- Leverage up to 1:30. Rewards from day one on simulated live, then 7-day
  cycle. No time limits.
--- 5C. INSTANT FUNDING (CFD, 5K-100K Master to $100K) ---
- No evaluation, no profit target. Simulated live from day one.
- Drawdown: 6% RELATIVE maximum + 6% daily. No consistency gate.
- Rewards from day one, then every 7 days. No time limits.
--- 5D. PRESTIGE (CFD/Futures, custom tiers) ---
- Top-tier path with custom targets/drawdown per tier; split to 100%.
  Terms issued per-tier at purchase — read the tier card, not this file.
--- 5E. FUTURES ARM ---
- Futures contracts offered alongside CFDs (per CFD + Futures positioning).
  Targets/drawdown follow the hosting program card (Prestige/futures tiers);
  data fees, contract limits and session rules confirmed at checkout.

SECTION 8 — PROHIBITIONS & TRADING RESTRICTIONS (official, condensed)
================================================================================
Source: FT+ Terms + Help Center + audit rulebooks.
BANNED (trade removal, disqualification, reward forfeiture or termination):
1. Account sharing / third-party trading: only the purchaser may trade;
   no managing others' accounts, no letting others trade yours.
2. Third-party signal-copying as decision-maker; copying across YOUR OWN
   FT+ accounts is permitted within risk rules.
3. Exploiting errors: price-feed errors, stale/slow feeds, latency arbitrage,
   platform-error abuse.
4. Coordinated manipulation incl. hedging across connected accounts/other
   firms/other people; opposite positions inside ONE account permitted.
5. Unfair-tech edge: HFT/latency/mass-order tools overloading the server.
6. Gambling/one-sided all-or-nothing risk inconsistent with replicable risk
   management (reviewed case-by-case).
7. Consistency-gate circumvention on Classic (spreading one idea across days
   to dodge the 35%/50% gate).
ALLOWED: news trading, weekend/overnight holding, EAs/bots (per audit).
Permissions never override the daily/max-loss hard breach lines.

SECTION 11 — COMPARISON NOTES & TRADER FIT
================================================================================
VS INDUSTRY: cheapest subscription entry ($119/mo evals) with day-one Instant
payouts and a $2.5-5M scaling ceiling — the highest audited cap in its
comparison set. Subscription suits fast passers; slow passers pay more than
one-time-fee rivals. Splits to 100% offset cost for profitable traders.
CLASSIC VS EXPRESS: Classic = two 7% phases, 1:50 leverage, 10-day payout
rhythm, 35%/50% consistency gates — for steady two-phase traders. Express =
single 10% phase, 1:30 leverage, day-one/7-day payouts — for confident
one-shot traders comfortable with tighter leverage.
INSTANT: for experienced traders skipping evaluation; 6%/6% risk box with
day-one rewards justifies $299/mo if first-month profitable.
BEST FIT: forex/indices/commodities/crypto CFD traders wanting cheap entry +
day-one payouts; patient Growth scalers to $2.5-5M; multi-platform traders;
180-country coverage.
BAD FIT: leverage-hungry traders (>1:50 needed); traders wanting one-time
fees or free trials; HFT/latency/gap-news styles; banned jurisdictions;
anyone needing guaranteed live (not simulated) capital.
