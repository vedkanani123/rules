SECTION 5 — MODEL-BY-MODEL RULES
================================================================================
--- 5A. INSTANT FUNDING ---
- Profit target: none; already funded from day one.
- Daily drawdown: 3-5% from prior-day close including floating; resets 17:00 NY.
- Max drawdown: 6-10% static floor from starting balance; breach ends account.
- First payout 14 days after first trade, then rolling 14-day cycle.
- Payout consistency: best profitable day must be <=50% of total profit in
  payout period or request waits (never breaches, never removes profit).
- Weekend holding of non-crypto over weekend may need Weekend Holding add-on;
  crypto exempt. Overnight needs Swap-Free add-on or be flat at 17:00 NY close.
- Funded news restriction: no opening/adding inside high-impact window, no
  holding through release. HFT prohibited on Instant.
--- 5B. 1-STEP EVALUATION ---
- Single evaluation target per plan card; on final required day, hitting target
  intraday lets you close and progress immediately.
- Daily + max drawdown as above (static max). Losing day alone never fails.
- Minimum profitable days per plan card (NY day, positive net closed profit,
  no percentage threshold), auto-counted in dashboard.
- Challenge news trading allowed during evaluation.
- Same 50% best-day payout-consistency rule as Instant once funded.
- Single-Trade Profit Dependency <=60% and One-Sided Concentration <=70%
  apply on every plan (see 5D).
--- 5C. 2-STEP (STANDARD + PRO) AND 3-STEP ---
- Staged targets (2-Step splits into two smaller targets; 3-Step into three).
  Same daily/static-max mechanics and min-days logic as 1-Step.
- No payout-consistency rule on 2-Step during evaluation or funded.
- 3-Step keeps news trading even when funded (per FAQ); other funded accounts
  restrict news-window opens/adds/holds.
- Warning history cleared on passing to funded; fresh funded credentials.
--- 5D. CROSS-CUTTING RISK RULES (all plans) ---
- Warning system: soft rules (max exposure, HFT, floating loss per trade)
  give formal warning, trading suspended, free Challenge Reset required
  (closes positions, restores starting balance, restarts progress, keeps
  warnings). Two warnings on one rule breaches permanently. Each rule counted
  separately. Funded Reset is paid, clears warnings, only while max-DD usage
  below 10%.
- Trade Consistency: latest closed trade <100% increase over prior-20 average
  (below double); opens after 5 closed trades; recorded at payout, never
  breaches, never changes split.
- Single-Trade Profit Dependency: largest winner <=60% of total net realised;
  opens after 10 trades + 2% net; re-checked at target/payout; first episode
  warning, second breach.
- One-Sided Concentration: per symbol after 10 closed trades, same-side trade
  share and exposure share each <=70%; both over = warning + 5-trade
  rebalancing window.
- Banned: news straddling, latency/tick arbitrage, stale-price exploits,
  realised-loss recovery over-sizing, group/multi-account coordination.
  Scalping welcome. EAs allowed only if genuinely self-built and controllable;
  no third-party/shared masters, signals, or passing services. Copy between
  own accounts allowed.
- Max open exposure per account shown live in dashboard Exposure calculator.
