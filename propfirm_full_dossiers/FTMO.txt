SECTION 5 — MODEL-BY-MODEL RULES
================================================================================
--- 5A. FTMO CHALLENGE 1-STEP (CFD) ---
- Profit target: 10% of initial balance, Challenge phase ONLY. No target on
  FTMO Account. Must close all positions to book the pass. ($100K: +$10,000.)
- Maximum Daily Loss: 3% of INITIAL balance. Limit = midnight CE(S)T balance
  minus 3% of initial. Recalculated daily 00:00 CE(S)T. Equity-based (balance +
  open P/L +/- swaps - commissions). Breach = fail. Applies on Challenge AND
  FTMO Account. ($100K: $3,000/day. Day1 limit $97,000; Day2 with $102,000
  midnight balance = $99,000 limit.)
- Maximum Loss: 10% EOD-TRAILING. Limit = highest midnight balance seen so far
  (or initial, if higher) minus 10% of initial. Trails UP only, never down.
  Resets fully on each new post-Reward FTMO Account (back to 90% of initial).
  ($100K: Day1 $90,000; Day2 after $104K close = $94,000; Day3 after $103K
  close stays $94,000.)
- Best-Day Rule (Challenge + FTMO Account): best single day (closed P/L) must
  be <=50% of Positive Days' Profit (sum of all profitable days). NOT a breach
  — you keep trading until ratio is fixed. Shown in Account MetriX.
  Example (official): days +10K/-2K/-2K/-2K/+6K => positive sum $16,000, best
  day $10,000 = 62.5% => must grow positive sum to >=$20,000.
- Minimum trading days: NONE.
- News: no restriction during Evaluation. On FTMO Account (Standard type):
  NO opening/closing within 2 minutes around restricted high-impact news on
  affected symbols (see 5E). Swing n/a (no Swing on 1-Step).
- Weekend/overnight: no restriction during Evaluation. On FTMO Account
  Standard: must close before weekend/overnight windows (see 5E).
- EA/copy/HFT: EAs allowed incl. third-party, BUT duplicate third-party EA
  strategies across traders risk max-capital-allocation denial; >2,000 server
  requests/day = hyperactivity warning; HFT/latency/data-feed exploits banned.
  Copy-trading YOUR OWN accounts is fine; trading others' accounts or letting
  others trade yours is banned (see Section 8).
--- 5B. FTMO CHALLENGE 2-STEP (CFD, Standard + Swing) ---
- Profit target: 10% Challenge Phase 1, 5% Verification Phase 2, none on FTMO
  Account. ($100K: $110,000 then $105,000, all positions closed.)
- Maximum Daily Loss: 5% of INITIAL balance, same midnight-recalc mechanics as
  1-Step. Applies Challenge + Verification + FTMO Account.
  ($100K: $5,000/day. Day1 $95,000; Day2 after $102K = $97,000.)
- Maximum Loss: 10% STATIC. Limit = initial minus 10%, fixed forever
  (no trailing). ($100K: $90,000 flat.)
- Minimum Trading Days: 4 per phase (Challenge AND Verification), none on
  FTMO Account. A day counts if >=1 position OPENED 00:00:00-23:59:59 CE(S)T.
  (Official example: opens on 12th, 18th, 18th, 24th = only 3 days => fail.)
- Consistency/Best-Day: NONE on 2-Step.
- News (funded Standard only): same 2-minute restricted-news rule as 1-Step.
  Swing: no news restriction at all. Evaluation: no restriction either type.
- Weekend/overnight (funded Standard only): positions must be closed per
  weekend/overnight rule; Swing: hold freely. Evaluation: hold freely.
- EA/copy/HFT: same as 1-Step.
--- 5C. FTMO FUTURES GROWTH (Beta, 50K/100K/150K) ---
- Profit target (Evaluation only): $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000 (balance > initial
  + target, positions closed). None on Sim-Funded.
- Max Drawdown: EOD-TRAILING $2,000 / $3,500 / $5,000. Calculated at start of
  each trading day (day = 6:00pm ET to 4:10pm ET next day) off highest prior
  close (or initial). Trails up only; LOCKS at initial once reached.
  Breach in Evaluation = fail (new account next billing date); on Sim-Funded =
  termination. Equity = balance + open P&L - commissions; equity TOUCHING the
  limit = breach.
- Daily Loss Limit: NONE in Evaluation. SOFT $1,000/$2,000/$3,000 on Sim-Funded
  (soft = trading paused, not auto-terminated — per third-party rule tables).
- Consistency (Evaluation only): best day <=40% of TOTAL profit (all days net).
  Not a breach; keep trading till ratio fixed. None on Sim-Funded.
- Max contracts (Evaluation): 5 / 10 / 15 (1 std/mini = 1; 10 micros = 1).
  Sim-Funded scales 2->5 / 4->10 / 6->15 with EOD profit milestones.
- News: allowed. No intraday trailing. No activation fee. No consistency rule
  on Sim-Funded.
--- 5D. FTMO FUTURES PRO (Beta, 50K/100K/150K) ---
- Same targets ($3K/$6K/$9K) and EOD-trailing mechanics as Growth, but WIDER
  drawdown: $3,000 / $4,500 / $6,000.
- Daily Loss Limit: HARD $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 in Evaluation AND Sim-Funded.
  Limit = start-of-day balance minus amount; can move up AND down day to day.
  Touching it = fail/termination.
- Consistency (Evaluation only): best day <=50% of total profit (looser than
  Growth's 40%). Not a breach. None on Sim-Funded.
- Max contracts: same 5/10/15. Withdrawals: 100% of profit (vs 50% Growth).
  Higher payout caps (see Section 7).
--- 5E. NEWS / WEEKEND / OVERNIGHT (CFD FUNDED STANDARD — verbatim logic) ---
- "Restrictions for trading during selected high-impact news apply only to the
  Standard account type" and "only once you start trading on an FTMO Account.
  They do not apply during the Evaluation Process." Swing has no restrictions.
- Standard funded rule: no opening/closing positions on the affected symbol
  from 2 minutes before until 2 minutes after the restricted release; affected
  instruments published in FTMO calendar/FAQ. Breach can forfeit profits of
  those trades and repeated abuse risks the account.
- Standard funded: positions must be closed before weekend close and within
  overnight restricted windows (crypto weekend hours vary by platform
  maintenance); Swing: hold over weekends/overnight freely.
- Futures: news trading allowed on both Growth and Pro; flat-to-close times

SECTION 8 — PROHIBITIONS & TRADING RESTRICTIONS (official, condensed)
================================================================================
Source: https://ftmo.com/en/forbidden-trading-practices/ (+ instruments FAQ).
BANNED (breach can mean trade removal, platform restriction, disqualification,
Reward forfeiture, or full termination):
1. Exploiting platform/data errors: price-display errors, stale/slow feeds,
   latency arbitrage.
2. Coordinated manipulation incl. across connected accounts / other firms /
   other people: e.g. opposite positions across accounts (hedging across
   accounts). Exception: opposite positions inside ONE single account are OK.
3. Violating FTMO General T&Cs, FTMO Account Agreement, or platform T&Cs.
4. Unfair-tech edge: software/AI/ultra-high-speed/mass-data-entry tools that
   manipulate or grant unfair advantage.
5. Gap trading: opening trades around scheduled major news/earnings affecting
   the market, or within 2 hours before a market closes for 2+ hours.
6. Non-replicable "real market" abuse: overleveraging, overexposure, one-sided
   bets, account rolling/gambling (detailed in FTMO gambling blog).
7. Hyperactive EAs: >2,000 server requests/day (order open/modify/close) that
   overload the server.
8. Best-Day circumvention: artificially spreading one trade idea across days
   (hedging same/highly-correlated symbols, partial-close management across
   days) to dodge the Best-Day/consistency rule.
RISK-MGMT RULES (must be replicable with own money): no substantially larger
sizes vs your other trades, no abnormal position counts, no stacked cumulative
exposure per symbol/correlated basket ("Risk per Trade Idea").
ACCOUNT SHARING (personal use only): nobody else may trade your account; you
may not trade anyone else's; no signal-copying someone else's trades onto your
FTMO account as the decision-maker; legal entities: only top management may
access. Third-party EAs used by many traders at once risk allocation denial.
Consequences ladder: conflicting-position removal, rebalance, leverage cut,
disqualification, Reward loss, termination of all agreements.

SECTION 11 — COMPARISON NOTES & TRADER FIT
================================================================================
VS INDUSTRY: the reference 2-Step benchmark (most-proven payout record). Fees
mid-range (cheaper than The Funded Trader, pricier than Apex promos) but 90%
top split offsets cost for profitable traders. Unlimited-time evaluation is
rarer and valuable vs 30/60-day rivals.
1-STEP VS 2-STEP: 1-Step is cheaper at every size ($41 less at 100K), has no
min days, starts at 90% — but daily room is tighter (3% vs 5%), drawdown
TRAILS (vs static 10%), Best-Day-50% applies on funded too, and fee is never
refunded. 2-Step costs more and needs 4 days/phase but gives 5% daily room,
static 10% max loss, zero consistency rule, fee refund, and Swing freedom.
Rule of thumb: news/swing/overnight traders -> 2-Step Swing. Conservative
intraday scalpers wanting max split fast -> 1-Step.
FUTURES GROWTH VS PRO: Growth = cheaper, no eval daily stop, but tighter DD
and 40% consistency + 50%-withdrawal + lower caps. Pro = +$20-40/mo, wider DD,
looser 50% consistency, 100% withdrawals, 2x caps — but a hard daily stop can
kill the account in one session. Lumpy-intraday traders -> Growth; steady
risk-capped traders -> Pro.
BEST FIT: forex/indices/commodities CFD traders wanting a 10-year proven
payer; patient traders using unlimited time; EAs (self-built); swing traders
(2-Step Swing); futures day traders fine with EOD-trailing + caps.
BAD FIT: traders needing >$200K day-one CFD capital; HFT/latency-gap-news
styles; anyone needing a guaranteed Live futures account; traders in banned
jurisdictions; traders who cannot survive a 3%-daily / Best-Day regime.
